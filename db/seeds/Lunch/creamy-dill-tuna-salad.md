# Creamy Dill Tuna Salad

A protein-rich, low-carb tuna salad featuring fresh dill and crunchy vegetables, perfect for serving over lettuce leaves

## Source
How Not To Get Sick

## Servings
4 servings

## Prep Time
15 minutes

## Cook Time
0 minutes

## Ingredients

- 2 (6-ounce) cans albacore tuna in water
- 2 (6-ounce) cans albacore tuna in olive oil
- 2 1/3 cup mayonnaise
- 2 tablespoons yellow mustard
- 1 tablespoon chopped fresh dill
- 1 tablespoon lime juice
- 1/3 cup thinly sliced celery
- 1/4 cup finely diced red onion
- 1/4 cup diced pickle
- 1/4 teaspoon salt
- 1/8 teaspoon black pepper
- 1 head butter lettuce
- Alfalfa sprouts, for garnish (optional)

## Instructions

1. Drain the cans of tuna and flake the tuna into a large bowl.

2. Stir in the mayonnaise, mustard, dill, lime juice, celery, red onion, pickle, salt, and pepper.

3. Mix until fully combined.

4. Cover and chill until serving.

5. Serve on top of large lettuce leaves and garnish with alfalfa sprouts if desired.

## Tips

- For best texture, drain the tuna thoroughly and gently flake with a fork rather than mashing it
- Chilling the salad for at least 30 minutes allows the flavors to meld and enhances the taste
- Choose crisp butter lettuce leaves as cups for an elegant presentation and extra crunch
