# Spinach Salad with Bacon and Poppy Seed Dressing

A fresh spinach salad topped with crispy bacon, caramelized shallots, Asian pear, pepitas, and goat cheese, served with a tangy poppy seed dressing

## Source
How Not To Get Sick

## Servings
4 servings

## Prep Time
15 minutes

## Cook Time
13 minutes

## Ingredients

### Salad
- 4 slices bacon
- 3 shallots, thinly sliced
- 8 ounces baby spinach
- Salt and black pepper to taste
- 1 Asian pear, cored and sliced
- 2 tablespoons roasted salted pepitas
- 1/4 cup crumbled goat cheese

### Poppy Seed Dressing
- 1/4 cup olive oil
- 2 tablespoons white balsamic vinegar
- 2 tablespoons granulated Swerve
- 1/2 teaspoon ground mustard
- 1/2 teaspoon poppy seeds
- 1 teaspoon grated white onion
- Pinch salt

## Instructions

1. Heat a skillet over medium-high heat. Add the bacon and cook until browned and crispy, about 6 minutes. Using a slotted spoon, transfer the bacon to a paper towel-lined plate, leaving the bacon drippings in the pot. When cool enough to handle, crumble the bacon.

2. Reduce the heat to medium-low. Add the shallots and cook until crispy, about 20 minutes, keeping a close eye to ensure that the shallots don't burn. Transfer the shallots to the paper towel-lined plate with the bacon.

3. Combine the dressing ingredients in a jar with a tight-fitting lid. Secure the lid and shake the jar to combine the ingredients.

4. Put the spinach in a large salad bowl and toss with some of the dressing. Season with salt and pepper.

5. Top with the sliced pear, pepitas, goat cheese, crispy shallots, and crumbled bacon.

## Tips

- Watch the shallots carefully during cooking to prevent burning - they should be golden and crispy, not blackened
- Save any extra poppy seed dressing in the refrigerator for up to one week for future salads
- For best results, add the dressing gradually to avoid overdressing the delicate spinach leaves
