# BLT Wedge Salad

A crisp iceberg lettuce wedge topped with chunky blue cheese dressing, juicy grape tomatoes and crunchy bacon

## Source
The Easy 5-Ingredient Ketogenic Diet Cookbook 

## Servings
2 servings

## Prep Time
10 minutes

## Cook Time
10 minutes

## Ingredients

- 4 bacon slices
- 1/2 head iceberg lettuce, halved
- 2 tablespoons blue cheese salad dressing
- 1/4 cup blue cheese crumbles
- 1/2 cup halved grape tomatoes

## Instructions

1. In a large skillet over medium-high heat, cook the bacon on both sides until crispy, about 8 minutes. Transfer the bacon to a paper towel-lined plate to drain and cool for 5 minutes. Transfer to a cutting board, and chop the bacon.

2. Place the lettuce wedges on two plates. Top each with half of the blue cheese dressing, the blue cheese crumbles, the halved grape tomatoes, and the chopped bacon, and serve.

## Tips

- If you have a grill, you can drizzle each iceberg lettuce wedge with 1 tablespoon of olive oil, season with salt and pepper, and grill each side for about 1 minute to add smoky flavor
- Make sure to drain the bacon on paper towels to remove excess grease for the best texture
- Use chunky blue cheese dressing for better flavor and texture contrast with the crisp lettuce
