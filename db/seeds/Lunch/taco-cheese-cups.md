# Taco Cheese Cups

Versatile and easy-to-make cheese cups filled with flavorful beef, fresh avocado, and cool sour cream. A delicious keto-friendly twist on traditional tacos with a crunchy cheese shell.

## Source
The Easy 5-Ingredient Ketogenic Diet Cookbook 

## Servings
2 servings

## Prep Time
10 minutes

## Cook Time
20 minutes

## Ingredients

### For the Cheese Cups
- 2 cups shredded cheese (Mexican blend preferred)

### For the Ground Beef
- 1 tablespoon ghee
- 1/2 pound ground beef
- 1/2 (1.25-ounce) package taco seasoning
- 1/4 cup water

### For the Taco Cups
- 1/2 avocado, diced
- Pink Himalayan salt to taste
- Freshly ground black pepper to taste
- 2 tablespoons sour cream

## Instructions

1. Preheat the oven to 350°F. Line a baking sheet with parchment paper or a silicone baking mat.

2. Place 1/2-cup mounds of the cheese on the prepared pan. Bake for about 7 minutes, or until the edges are brown and the middle has melted.

3. Put the pan on a cooling rack for 2 minutes while the cheese chips cool. The chips will be floppy when they first come out of the oven, but they will begin to crisp as they cool.

4. Before they are fully crisp, move the cheese chips to a muffin tin. Form the cheese chips around the shape of the muffin cups to create small bowls.

5. In a medium skillet over medium-high heat, heat the ghee.

6. When the ghee is hot, add the ground beef and sauté for about 8 minutes, until browned.

7. Drain the excess grease. Stir in the taco seasoning and water, and bring to a boil. Turn the heat to medium-low and simmer for 5 minutes.

8. Using a slotted spoon, spoon the ground beef into the taco cups.

9. Season the diced avocado with pink Himalayan salt and pepper, and divide it among the taco cups.

10. Add a dollop of sour cream to each taco cup and serve.

## Tips

- The cheese chips will be floppy when they first come out of the oven but will crisp as they cool - shape them into cups before they fully harden.
- Taco cups will keep well for 2 to 3 days in a sealed container in the refrigerator.
- Try using pepper Jack cheese for an extra kick, or add diced tomatoes, onions, and jalapeños to the ground beef for more flavor variations.
