# Chopped Greek Salad

A fresh and easy Greek salad with just a few ingredients that makes a perfect restaurant-style dish

## Source
The Easy 5-Ingredient Ketogenic Diet Cookbook 

## Servings
2 servings

## Prep Time
10 minutes

## Cook Time
0 minutes

## Ingredients

- 2 cups chopped romaine lettuce
- 1/2 cup halved grape tomatoes
- 1/4 cup sliced black olives (like Kalamata)
- 1/4 cup feta cheese crumbles
- 2 tablespoons vinaigrette salad dressing
- Pink Himalayan salt to taste
- Freshly ground black pepper to taste
- 1 tablespoon olive oil

## Instructions

1. In a large bowl, combine the romaine, tomatoes, olives, feta cheese, and vinaigrette.

2. Season with pink Himalayan salt and pepper, drizzle with the olive oil, and toss to combine.

3. Divide the salad between two bowls and serve.

## Tips

- You can substitute feta cheese with goat cheese for a different flavor profile
- Add red onion, finely chopped cucumbers, or pepperoncini for extra crunch and freshness
- For a heartier version, try adding finely chopped Genoa salami or pepperoni
