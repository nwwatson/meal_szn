# Crispy Parmesan Crackers

Lacy, golden crackers made from melted Parmesan cheese and butter that create a crispy, savory keto-friendly treat

## Source
The Complete Ketogenic Guide for Beginners 

## Servings
8 crackers

## Prep Time
10 minutes

## Cook Time
5 minutes

## Ingredients

- 1 teaspoon butter
- 8 ounces full-fat Parmesan cheese, shredded or freshly grated

## Instructions

1. Preheat the oven to 400°F.

2. Line a baking sheet with parchment paper and lightly grease the paper with the butter.

3. Spoon the Parmesan cheese onto the baking sheet in mounds, spread evenly apart.

4. Spread out the mounds with the back of a spoon until they are flat.

5. Bake the crackers until the edges are browned and the centers are still pale, about 5 minutes.

6. Remove the sheet from the oven, and remove the crackers with a spatula to paper towels. Lightly blot the tops with additional paper towels and let them completely cool.

7. Store in a sealed container in the refrigerator for up to 4 days.

## Tips

- Use freshly grated or shredded Parmesan cheese rather than pre-grated supermarket varieties, as they tend to be too dry and powdery to melt properly
- Make sure to space the cheese mounds evenly apart on the baking sheet as they will spread significantly during baking
- Blot the finished crackers with paper towels to remove excess oil and ensure maximum crispiness
