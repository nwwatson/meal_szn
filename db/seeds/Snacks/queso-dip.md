# Queso Dip

Also known as chile con queso, this dip originated in Mexico and can be found in many places that serve Tex-Mex cuisine. A creamy, spicy cheese dip perfect for keto crackers or low-carb vegetables.

## Source
The Complete Ketogenic Guide for Beginners 

## Servings
6 servings

## Prep Time
5 minutes

## Cook Time
10 minutes

## Ingredients

- 1/4 cup coconut milk
- 1/2 jalapeño pepper, seeded and diced
- 1 teaspoon minced garlic
- 2 ounces goat cheese
- 6 ounces sharp Cheddar cheese, shredded
- 1/4 teaspoon cayenne pepper
- 1 1/2 teaspoon onion powder

## Instructions

1. Place a medium pot over medium heat and add the coconut milk, jalapeño, garlic, and onion powder.

2. Bring the liquid to a simmer and then whisk in the goat cheese until smooth.

3. Add the Cheddar cheese and cayenne and whisk until the dip is thick, 30 seconds to 1 minute.

4. Pour into a serving dish and serve with keto crackers or low-carb vegetables.

## Tips

- For a spicier dip, use a hotter pepper like habanero or Scotch bonnet chile, which rank higher on the Scoville scale than jalapeños.
- Keep the heat at medium to prevent the cheese from breaking or becoming grainy.
- Serve immediately while warm for the best texture and consistency.
