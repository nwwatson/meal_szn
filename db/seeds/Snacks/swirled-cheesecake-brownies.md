# Swirled Cheesecake Brownies

Decadent keto-friendly brownies with a marbled cream cheese layer, sweetened with sugar-free alternatives

## Source
How Not To Get Sick

## Servings
20 servings

## Prep Time
15 minutes

## Cook Time
30 minutes

## Ingredients

### Chocolate Layer
- 12 tablespoons (1 1/2 sticks) unsalted butter
- 1/2 cup sugar-free semisweet chocolate chips
- 1/2 cup unsweetened cocoa powder
- 1 cup granular Swerve
- 1/2 cup brown Swerve
- 2 large eggs plus 1 large egg yolk
- 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
- 1/2 teaspoon salt
- 1 cup almond flour

### Cheesecake Layer
- 8 ounces cream cheese, room temperature
- 1/4 cup granular Swerve
- 1 large egg
- 1/2 teaspoon vanilla extract

## Instructions

1. Preheat the oven to 350 degrees F. Line a 7 x 11-inch baking pan with parchment paper.

2. For the chocolate layer, pour about an inch of water into a small saucepan and bring to a simmer. Put the butter and chocolate chips in a heatproof bowl and set it on top of the saucepan, making sure the bottom of the bowl does not touch the water below.

3. Stir the butter and chocolate together until melted and smooth. Remove the bowl from the heat.

4. Mix in the cocoa powder, then the granular and brown Swerve, eggs and yolk (one at a time), vanilla, salt, and flour. Measure out 1/2 cup of the batter. Pour the rest into the prepared pan.

5. For the cheesecake layer: In the bowl of an electric mixer, beat the cream cheese and Swerve on medium speed until smooth. Add the egg and vanilla and beat until smooth.

6. Pour the cream cheese mixture over the brownie mixture in the pan. Dot the top with the reserved batter.

7. Use a butter knife to swirl the batter into the cream cheese layer to make a marbled effect.

8. Bake for about 30 minutes, until the center has set. Cool completely before cutting and serving.

## Tips

- Make sure cream cheese is at room temperature for smooth mixing and to prevent lumps in the cheesecake layer
- Cool completely before cutting to ensure clean slices and prevent the layers from separating
- Store covered in the refrigerator for up to 5 days for best texture and freshness
