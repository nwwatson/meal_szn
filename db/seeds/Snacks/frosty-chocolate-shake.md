# "Frosty" Chocolate Shake

A keto-friendly copycat recipe of Wendy's Frosty that delivers the same creamy, chocolatey satisfaction without the carbs

## Source
The Easy 5-Ingredient Ketogenic Diet Cookbook 

## Servings
2 servings

## Prep Time
10 minutes

## Cook Time
0 minutes

## Ingredients

- 3/4 cup heavy whipping cream
- 4 ounces coconut milk
- 1 tablespoon Swerve natural sweetener
- 1/4 teaspoon vanilla extract
- 2 tablespoons unsweetened cocoa powder

## Instructions

1. Pour the cream into a medium cold metal bowl, and with your hand mixer and cold beaters, beat the cream just until it forms peaks.

2. Slowly pour in the coconut milk, and gently stir it into the cream. Add the sweetener, vanilla, and cocoa powder, and beat until fully combined.

3. Pour into two tall glasses, and chill in the freezer for 1 hour before serving. Stir the shakes twice during this time.

## Tips

- Chill a medium metal mixing bowl and hand-mixer beaters in the freezer while preparing to make this recipe for best results
- Use part of a 13.5-ounce can of organic coconut milk and stir it up after opening for optimal consistency
- Almond milk can be substituted for coconut milk if needed
