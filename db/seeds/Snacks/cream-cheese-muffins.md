# Cream Cheese Muffins

Delicious keto-friendly muffins made with cream cheese and heavy cream, creating a rich and fluffy texture perfect for breakfast or snacks.

## Source
The Easy 5-Ingredient Ketogenic Diet Cookbook 

## Servings
6 muffins

## Prep Time
10 minutes

## Cook Time
12 minutes

## Ingredients

- 4 tablespoons melted butter, plus more for the muffin tin
- 1 cup almond flour
- 3/4 tablespoon baking powder
- 2 large eggs, lightly beaten
- 2 ounces cream cheese mixed with 2 tablespoons heavy whipping cream
- Handful shredded Mexican blend cheese

## Instructions

1. Preheat the oven to 400°F. Coat six cups of a muffin tin with butter.

2. In a small bowl, mix together the almond flour and baking powder.

3. In a medium bowl, mix together the eggs, cream cheese-heavy cream mixture, shredded cheese, and 4 tablespoons of the melted butter.

4. Pour the flour mixture into the egg mixture, and beat with a hand mixer until thoroughly mixed.

5. Pour the batter into the prepared muffin cups.

6. Bake for 12 minutes, or until golden brown on top, and serve.

## Tips

- You can use almond meal instead of almond flour - the mix will just have more texture because almond meal is less refined.
- Add savory Everything But the Bagel Seasoning on top prior to baking for a salty, sesame-seed flavor and texture.
- For sweet variations, try adding 1 tablespoon grated lemon zest and a handful of blueberries, or add diced jalapeños for a spicy version.
