# Bacon-Wrapped Jalapeños

Easy three-ingredient game day appetizer featuring jalapeños stuffed with cream cheese and wrapped in crispy bacon

## Source
The Easy 5-Ingredient Ketogenic Diet Cookbook 

## Servings
4 servings

## Prep Time
10 minutes

## Cook Time
20 minutes

## Ingredients

- 10 jalapeños
- 8 ounces cream cheese, at room temperature
- 1 pound bacon (you will use about half a slice per popper)

## Instructions

1. Preheat the oven to 450°F. Line a baking sheet with aluminum foil or a silicone baking mat.

2. Halve the jalapeños lengthwise, and remove the seeds and membranes (if you like the extra heat, leave them in). Place them on the prepared pan cut-side up.

3. Spread some of the cream cheese inside each jalapeño half.

4. Wrap a jalapeño half with a slice of bacon (depending on the size of the jalapeño, use a whole slice of bacon, or half).

5. Secure the bacon around each jalapeño with 1 to 2 toothpicks so it stays put while baking.

6. Bake for 20 minutes, until the bacon is done and crispy.

7. Serve hot or at room temperature. Either way, they are delicious!

## Tips

- Wear thin rubber gloves when prepping fresh jalapeños as the capsaicin soaks into your skin and can be irritating even after washing hands multiple times
- Perfect for game day prep - put family members to work preparing the ingredients while you watch the game
- Adjust bacon amount based on jalapeño size - larger peppers may need a full slice while smaller ones only need half a slice
