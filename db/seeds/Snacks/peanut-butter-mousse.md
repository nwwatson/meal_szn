# Peanut Butter Mousse

A scrumptious keto-friendly dessert made with natural peanut butter and heavy cream, high in healthy unsaturated fats and protein

## Source
The Complete Ketogenic Guide for Beginners 

## Servings
4 servings

## Prep Time
10 minutes

## Cook Time
30 minutes chilling

## Ingredients

- 1 cup heavy whipping cream
- 1/4 cup natural peanut butter
- 1 teaspoon alcohol-free pure vanilla extract
- 4 drops liquid stevia

## Instructions

1. In a medium bowl, beat together the heavy cream, peanut butter, vanilla, and stevia until firm peaks form, about 5 minutes.

2. Spoon the mousse into 4 bowls and place in the refrigerator to chill for 30 minutes.

3. Serve.

## Tips

- Make sure to use natural peanut butter without added sugars to keep the carb count low
- Chill your bowl and beaters beforehand to help the cream whip faster and hold its shape better
- For best results, serve within 2 hours of chilling as the mousse is at its optimal texture
