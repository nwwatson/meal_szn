# Sweet Whipped Cream

A keto-friendly whipped cream made with powdered Swerve sweetener

## Source
How Not To Get Sick

## Servings
8 servings

## Prep Time
20 minutes

## Cook Time
0 minutes

## Ingredients

- 1 pint cold heavy cream
- 6 tablespoons powdered Swerve, or more to taste
- 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
- 1 pinch salt

## Instructions

1. Place the metal bowl and metal whisk attachment from an electric mixer in the freezer for 15 minutes.

2. Combine all of the ingredients in the chilled bowl and whisk on high until stiff peaks form, about 5 minutes.

3. Serve and enjoy immediately.

## Tips

- Make sure your heavy cream is very cold before whipping for best results
- Chilling the bowl and whisk attachment helps the cream whip faster and hold its shape better
- Be careful not to over-whip or you'll end up with butter - stop as soon as stiff peaks form
