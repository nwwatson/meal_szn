# Creamy Banana Fat Bombs

Banana-flavored sweet fat bombs with a creamy texture, perfect for a keto-friendly dessert with exotic and comforting flavors

## Source
The Complete Ketogenic Guide for Beginners 

## Servings
12 fat bombs

## Prep Time
10 minutes, plus 1 hour chilling time

## Cook Time
0 minutes

## Ingredients

- 1 1/4 cups cream cheese, at room temperature
- 3/4 cup heavy whipping cream
- 1 tablespoon pure banana extract
- 6 drops liquid stevia

## Instructions

1. Line a baking sheet with parchment paper and set aside.

2. In a medium bowl, beat together the cream cheese, heavy cream, banana extract, and stevia until smooth and very thick, about 5 minutes.

3. Gently spoon the mixture onto the baking sheet in mounds, leaving some space between each mound, and place the baking sheet in the refrigerator until firm, about 1 hour.

4. Store the fat bombs in an airtight container in the refrigerator for up to 1 week.

## Tips

- Make sure cream cheese is at room temperature for easier mixing and smoother texture
- You can substitute banana extract with vanilla or almond extract for different flavor variations
- Top with shredded toasted coconut for added texture and flavor enhancement
