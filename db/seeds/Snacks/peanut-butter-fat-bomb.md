# Peanut Butter Fat Bomb

Quick and easy keto fat bombs made with peanut butter, perfect for a sweet treat that can be made before dinner and ready for dessert

## Source
The Easy 5-Ingredient Ketogenic Diet Cookbook 

## Servings
2 servings

## Prep Time
10 minutes

## Cook Time
30 minutes freeze time

## Ingredients

- 1 tablespoon butter, at room temperature
- 1 tablespoon coconut oil
- 2 tablespoons all-natural peanut butter or almond butter
- 2 teaspoons Swerve natural sweetener or 2 drops liquid stevia

## Instructions

1. In a microwave-safe medium bowl, melt the butter, coconut oil, and peanut butter in the microwave on 50 percent power. Mix in the sweetener.

2. Pour the mixture into fat bomb molds. (Small silicone cupcake molds work well.)

3. Freeze for 30 minutes, unmold them, and eat! Keep some extras in your freezer so you can eat them anytime you are craving a sweet treat.

## Tips

- All ingredients should be at room temperature for best results - this is important for any fat bomb recipe
- You can use an ice cube tray as a fat bomb mold. When frozen, transfer to a zip-top bag and store in the freezer for up to 3 months
- These can be made before dinner and will be ready in time for dessert, making them perfect for quick sweet cravings
