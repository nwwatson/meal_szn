# Crustless Cheesecake Bites

Easy keto-friendly cheesecake bites that make perfect bite-size desserts for family dinners or dinner parties

## Source
The Easy 5-Ingredient Ketogenic Diet Cookbook 

## Servings
4 servings

## Prep Time
10 minutes (plus 3 hours to chill)

## Cook Time
30 minutes

## Ingredients

- 4 ounces cream cheese, at room temperature
- 1/4 cup sour cream
- 2 large eggs
- 1/3 cup Swerve natural sweetener
- 1/4 teaspoon vanilla extract

## Instructions

1. Preheat the oven to 350°F.

2. In a medium mixing bowl, use a hand mixer to beat the cream cheese, sour cream, eggs, sweetener, and vanilla until well mixed.

3. Place silicone liners (or cupcake paper liners) in the cups of a muffin tin.

4. Pour the cheesecake batter into the liners, and bake for 30 minutes.

5. Refrigerate until completely cooled before serving, about 3 hours.

## Tips

- Allow cream cheese to come to room temperature for easier mixing
- Store extra cheesecake bites in a zip-top bag in the freezer for up to 3 months
- Add lemon zest for a delicious burst of flavor, or make a keto key lime version by adding 1/2 teaspoon sugar-free lime Jello mix and 1/2 teaspoon fresh lime juice
