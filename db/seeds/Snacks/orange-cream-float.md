# Orange Cream Float

A refreshing keto-friendly float made with diet orange soda and heavy cream, perfect for enjoying orange flavor on a ketogenic diet.

## Source
The Easy 5-Ingredient Ketogenic Diet Cookbook 

## Servings
2 servings

## Prep Time
5 minutes

## Cook Time
0 minutes

## Ingredients

- 1 can diet orange soda (like Zevia's)
- 4 tablespoons heavy whipping cream
- 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
- 6 ice cubes

## Instructions

1. In a food processor or blender, combine the orange soda, heavy cream, vanilla, and ice.

2. Blend well, pour into two tall glasses, and serve.

## Tips

- For a boozy version, add vanilla vodka to the mix for an adult orange cream float.
- Use Zevia Orange Soda made with stevia for the best keto-friendly results.
- Serve immediately in tall glasses for the best texture and temperature.
