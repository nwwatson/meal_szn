# Blackberry Cheesecake Squares

Rich keto-friendly cheesecake squares with a toasted almond flour crust and swirled blackberry sauce

## Source
How Not To Get Sick

## Servings
16 servings

## Prep Time
20 minutes, plus 4 hours chilling time

## Cook Time
35 minutes

## Ingredients

### Crust
- 2 cups almond flour
- 1/4 cup confectioners Swerve
- 1 teaspoon ground cinnamon
- 1/4 teaspoon salt
- 8 tablespoons (1 stick) unsalted butter, melted

### Blackberry Sauce
- 2 cups fresh or frozen blackberries, divided
- 3 tablespoons water, divided
- 1 teaspoon granular Swerve
- 1 teaspoon cornstarch

### Cheesecake
- 16 ounces cream cheese, room temperature
- 1/2 cup granular Swerve
- 1/3 cup sour cream, room temperature
- 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
- 2 large eggs, room temperature, beaten
- 1/4 teaspoon grated lemon zest

## Instructions

1. Preheat the oven to 300 degrees F. Line an 8-inch baking pan with parchment paper.

2. To make the crust, heat a large skillet over medium heat. Add the almond flour and toast until golden, stirring often, about 5 minutes. Transfer the almond flour to a large bowl, add the confectioners Swerve, cinnamon, salt, and melted butter, and mix well. Press the crust into the bottom of the prepared pan.

3. To make the blackberry sauce, in a small saucepan, combine 1 1/2 cups of blackberries, 2 tablespoons of water, and the granular Swerve and cook over low heat until soft, about 10 minutes.

4. Strain the sauce through a fine-mesh strainer to remove the seeds, then return the juice to the pan. In a small bowl, whisk together the cornstarch and remaining 1 tablespoon of water. Add the mixture to the juice and simmer over low heat until slightly thickened, about 3 minutes. Set aside to cool.

5. To make the cheesecake, using the paddle attachment of an electric mixer, beat the cream cheese and granular Swerve until smooth. Add the sour cream and vanilla and beat until smooth.

6. Add the beaten eggs one at a time, beating until smooth. Add the lemon zest.

7. Pour the batter over the crust and spread evenly. Spoon the blackberry sauce in dollops over the top of the cheesecake. Use a toothpick to swirl in and out of each dollop to create a design. Arrange the remaining 1/2 cup of blackberries over the top.

8. Bake for 30-35 minutes, until the middle is mostly set but still slightly wiggly.

9. Turn off the oven, open the oven door, and let it sit for 25 minutes. Remove and cool to room temperature, then refrigerate for at least 4 hours before slicing and serving.

## Tips

- If blackberries aren't your favorite, feel free to omit the blackberry sauce and make New York-style cheesecake squares instead. Top with sliced fresh strawberries or a drizzle of chocolate ganache.
- Make sure all ingredients are at room temperature before mixing to ensure a smooth, creamy texture.
- The gradual cooling process (in the oven with door open, then at room temperature, then refrigerated) helps prevent cracks in the cheesecake surface.
