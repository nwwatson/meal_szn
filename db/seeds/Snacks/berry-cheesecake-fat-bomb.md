# Berry Cheesecake Fat Bomb

These fat bombs give you all the flavor of cheesecake in a small bite with a combination of mashed strawberries and blackberries folded into a creamy mixture.

## Source
The Easy 5-Ingredient Ketogenic Diet Cookbook 

## Servings
2 servings

## Prep Time
10 minutes

## Cook Time
0 minutes

## Ingredients

- 4 ounces cream cheese, at room temperature
- 4 tablespoons (1/2 stick) butter, at room temperature
- 2 teaspoons Swerve natural sweetener or 2 drops liquid stevia
- 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
- 1/4 cup berries, fresh or frozen

## Instructions

1. In a medium bowl, use a hand mixer to beat the cream cheese, butter, sweetener, and vanilla.

2. In a small bowl, mash the berries thoroughly. Fold the berries into the cream-cheese mixture using a rubber scraper.

3. Spoon the cream-cheese mixture into fat bomb molds. Use small silicone cupcake molds placed in the cups of a muffin tin, or just use cupcake papers if you don't have molds.

4. Freeze for at least 2 hours, unmold them, and eat! Leftover fat bombs can be stored in the freezer in a zip-top bag for up to 3 months.

## Tips

- All ingredients should be at room temperature for best results when making fat bombs
- Mash berries thoroughly before folding into the cream cheese mixture to prevent an off-putting texture when frozen
- You can use an ice cube tray as an easy fat bomb mold if you don't have specialized molds
