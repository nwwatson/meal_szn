# Chocolate Mousse

This dessert is rich, chocolatey, and super indulgent. It is packed with fats and bursting with creamy, chocolatey flavor.

## Source
The Easy 5-Ingredient Ketogenic Diet Cookbook 

## Servings
2 servings

## Prep Time
10 minutes

## Cook Time
0 minutes

## Ingredients

- 1 1/2 tablespoons heavy whipping cream
- 4 tablespoons butter, at room temperature
- 1 tablespoon unsweetened cocoa powder
- 4 tablespoons cream cheese, at room temperature
- 1 tablespoon Swerve natural sweetener

## Instructions

1. In a medium chilled bowl, use a whisk or fork to whip the cream. Refrigerate to keep cold.

2. In a separate medium bowl, use a hand mixer to beat the butter, cocoa powder, cream cheese, and sweetener until thoroughly combined.

3. Take the whipped cream out of the refrigerator. Gently fold the whipped cream into the chocolate mixture with a rubber scraper.

4. Divide the pudding between two dessert bowls.

5. Cover and chill for 1 hour before serving.

## Tips

- For making the whipping cream, leave a clean medium metal bowl in the freezer for a couple of hours before use. If using beaters, put those in the freezer to chill for a few minutes before whipping the cream.
- Fresh raspberries are delicious on top of this mousse for added flavor and texture.
- For extra-high-quality fat, add 1/4 of an avocado to this mix for smooth creaminess.
