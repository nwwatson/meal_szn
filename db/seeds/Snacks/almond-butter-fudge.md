# Almond Butter Fudge

A smooth and dense keto-friendly fudge made with almond butter and coconut oil, rich in protein, vitamin E, iron, manganese, and fiber

## Source
The Complete Ketogenic Guide for Beginners 

## Servings
18 servings (36 pieces)

## Prep Time
10 minutes

## Cook Time
2 hours chilling time

## Ingredients

- 1 cup coconut oil, at room temperature
- 1 cup almond butter
- 1/4 cup heavy whipping cream
- 10 drops liquid stevia
- Pinch sea salt

## Instructions

1. Line a 6-by-6-inch baking dish with parchment paper and set aside.

2. In a medium bowl, whisk together the coconut oil, almond butter, heavy cream, stevia, and salt until very smooth.

3. Spoon the mixture into the baking dish and smooth the top with a spatula.

4. Place the dish in the refrigerator until the fudge is firm, about 2 hours.

5. Cut into 36 pieces and store the fudge in an airtight container in the freezer for up to 2 weeks.

## Tips

- Make sure coconut oil is at room temperature for easy mixing and smooth texture
- You can substitute almond butter with peanut butter or cashew butter for different flavors
- Store in the freezer for firmer texture and longer shelf life up to 2 weeks
