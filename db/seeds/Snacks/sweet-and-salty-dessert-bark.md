# Sweet and Salty Dessert Bark

A rich chocolate bark made with coconut butter and cocoa powder, topped with nuts and sea salt for a perfect sweet and salty treat

## Source
How Not To Get Sick

## Servings
8 servings

## Prep Time
10 minutes, plus 20 minutes chilling time

## Cook Time
5 minutes

## Ingredients

- 1 cup coconut butter
- 1/2 cup unsweetened cocoa powder
- 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
- 1/3 cup confectioners Swerve, plus more to taste
- 1/2 teaspoon flaky sea salt
- 1 tablespoon pepitas
- 1 tablespoon chopped raw almonds
- 1 tablespoon shelled pistachios

## Instructions

1. Line a rimmed baking sheet with parchment paper.

2. Pour about an inch of water into a small saucepan and bring to a simmer.

3. Put the coconut butter in a heatproof bowl and set it on top of the saucepan, making sure the bottom of the bowl does not touch the water below. Stir until melted and smooth.

4. Remove the bowl from the heat and stir in the cocoa powder, vanilla, and Swerve.

5. Spread the melted chocolate mixture over the parchment paper on the baking sheet.

6. Sprinkle with the flaky salt, pepitas, almonds, and pistachios.

7. Chill until it hardens, about 20 minutes, then break the bark into pieces.

8. Store in an airtight container in the refrigerator or freezer.

## Tips

- Make sure the bowl doesn't touch the water when melting coconut butter to prevent overheating and seizing
- Taste the mixture before spreading and add more Swerve if you prefer a sweeter bark
- Store in the freezer for longer storage or if you prefer a firmer texture
