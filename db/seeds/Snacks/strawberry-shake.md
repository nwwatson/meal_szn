# Strawberry Shake

A strawberry cheesecake-inspired shake that's ready in minutes with no baking required

## Source
The Easy 5-Ingredient Ketogenic Diet Cookbook 

## Servings
2 servings

## Prep Time
10 minutes

## Cook Time
0 minutes

## Ingredients

- 3/4 cup heavy whipping cream
- 2 ounces cream cheese, at room temperature
- 1 tablespoon Swerve natural sweetener
- 1/4 teaspoon vanilla extract
- 6 strawberries, sliced
- 6 ice cubes

## Instructions

1. In a food processor (or blender), combine the heavy cream, cream cheese, sweetener, and vanilla. Mix on high to fully combine.

2. Add the strawberries and ice, and blend until smooth.

3. Pour into two tall glasses and serve.

## Tips

- A little swirl of whipped cream is always a nice topping to any milkshake
- Make sure cream cheese is at room temperature for easier blending and smoother texture
- For a thicker shake, use less ice cubes or add more frozen strawberries instead
