# Dark-Chocolate Strawberry Bark

A keto-friendly chocolate bark with a satisfying crunch from almonds and fresh strawberry slices

## Source
The Easy 5-Ingredient Ketogenic Diet Cookbook 

## Servings
2 servings

## Prep Time
10 minutes plus 2 hours to chill

## Cook Time
1 minute

## Ingredients

- 1/2 (2.8-ounce) keto-friendly chocolate bar (such as Lily's)
- 1 tablespoon heavy whipping cream
- 2 tablespoons salted almonds
- 1 fresh strawberry, sliced

## Instructions

1. Line a baking sheet with parchment paper.

2. Break up the chocolate bar half into small pieces, and put them in a microwave-safe bowl with the cream.

3. Heat in the microwave for 45 seconds at 50 percent power. Stir the chocolate, and cook for 20 seconds more at 50 percent power. Stir again, making sure the mixture is fully melted and combined. If not, microwave for another 20 seconds.

4. Pour the chocolate mixture onto the parchment paper and spread it in a thin, uniform layer.

5. Sprinkle on the almonds, then add the strawberry slices.

6. Refrigerate until hardened, about 2 hours.

7. Once the bark is nice and hard, break it up into smaller pieces to nibble on.

8. The bark will keep for up to 4 days in a sealed container in the refrigerator.

## Tips

- You can substitute macadamia nuts for the almonds for a delicious variation.
- Make sure to use 50 percent power when melting the chocolate to prevent burning and ensure smooth melting.
- Store the finished bark in the refrigerator in a sealed container to maintain its crisp texture.
