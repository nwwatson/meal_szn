# Cheese Chips and Guacamole

A keto-friendly appetizer featuring crispy baked cheese chips served with fresh guacamole, perfect for satisfying chip cravings on a ketogenic diet

## Source
The Easy 5-Ingredient Ketogenic Diet Cookbook 

## Servings
2 servings

## Prep Time
10 minutes

## Cook Time
10 minutes

## Ingredients

### For the Cheese Chips
- 1 cup shredded cheese (Mexican blend)

### For the Guacamole
- 1 avocado, mashed
- Juice of 1/2 lime
- 1 teaspoon diced jalapeño
- 2 tablespoons chopped fresh cilantro leaves
- Pink Himalayan salt to taste
- Freshly ground black pepper to taste

## Instructions

1. Preheat the oven to 350°F. Line a baking sheet with parchment paper or a silicone baking mat.

2. Add 1/4-cup mounds of shredded cheese to the pan, leaving plenty of space between them, and bake until the edges are brown and the middles have fully melted, about 7 minutes.

3. Set the pan on a cooling rack, and let the cheese chips cool for 5 minutes. The chips will be floppy when they first come out of the oven but will crisp as they cool.

4. In a medium bowl, mix together the avocado, lime juice, jalapeño, and cilantro, and season with pink Himalayan salt and pepper.

5. Top the cheese chips with the guacamole, and serve.

## Tips

- You can also add some of the diced jalapeños to the cheese mixture before baking the chips for extra spice
- Make sure to leave plenty of space between cheese mounds as they will spread while baking
- Let the cheese chips cool completely before serving as they will crisp up during the cooling process
