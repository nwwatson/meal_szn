# Root Beer Float

A keto-friendly version of the classic root beer float that's sugar-free and delicious

## Source
The Easy 5-Ingredient Ketogenic Diet Cookbook 

## Servings
2 servings

## Prep Time
5 minutes

## Cook Time
0 minutes

## Ingredients

- 1 (12-ounce) can diet root beer (Zevia's recommended)
- 4 tablespoons heavy whipping cream
- 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
- 6 ice cubes

## Instructions

1. In a food processor or blender, combine the root beer, cream, vanilla, and ice.

2. Blend well, pour into two tall glasses, and serve.

3. Serve with bendy straws for the complete root beer float experience.

## Tips

- For a boozy version, add vanilla vodka or rum to the mix
- Use Zevia brand root beer for the best sugar-free taste
- Serve immediately after blending for the best texture and foam
