# Strawberry Cheesecake Mousse

A super-easy 10-minute no-cook dessert that cheesecake lovers will devour, easily customizable with different fruits

## Source
The Easy 5-Ingredient Ketogenic Diet Cookbook 

## Servings
2 servings

## Prep Time
10 minutes

## Cook Time
0 minutes

## Ingredients

- 4 ounces cream cheese, at room temperature
- 1 tablespoon heavy whipping cream
- 1 teaspoon Swerve natural sweetener or 1 drop liquid stevia
- 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
- 4 strawberries, sliced (fresh or frozen)

## Instructions

1. Break up the cream cheese block into smaller pieces and distribute evenly in a food processor or blender. Add the cream, sweetener, and vanilla.

2. Mix together on high speed. Stop and stir twice, scraping down the sides of the bowl with a small rubber scraper to make sure everything is mixed well.

3. Add the strawberries to the food processor, and mix until combined.

4. Divide the strawberry cheesecake mixture between two small dishes, and chill for 1 hour before serving.

## Tips

- If the mixture seems too thick when mixing, add a little more heavy whipping cream to thin it out
- Try substituting blackberries (about 1/4 cup) for strawberries for a different flavor variation
- For a holiday twist, replace strawberries with 3 ounces pumpkin puree and 1 teaspoon pumpkin pie spice
