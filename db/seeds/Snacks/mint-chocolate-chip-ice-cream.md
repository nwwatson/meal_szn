# Mint-Chocolate Chip Ice Cream

A homemade low-carbohydrate ice cream with peppermint and sugar-free chocolate chips that doesn't require a fancy ice cream maker

## Source
The Easy 5-Ingredient Ketogenic Diet Cookbook 

## Servings
2 servings

## Prep Time
10 minutes, plus at least 4 hours to freeze

## Cook Time
30 minutes

## Ingredients

- 1/2 tablespoon butter
- 1 tablespoon Swerve natural sweetener
- 10 tablespoons heavy whipping cream, divided
- 1/4 teaspoon peppermint extract
- 2 tablespoons sugar-free chocolate chips

## Instructions

1. Put a medium metal bowl and your hand-mixer beaters in the freezer to chill.

2. In a small, heavy saucepan over medium heat, melt the butter. Whisk in the sweetener and 5 tablespoons of cream.

3. Turn the heat up to medium-high and bring the mixture to a boil, stirring constantly. Turn the heat down to low and simmer, stirring occasionally, for about 30 minutes. You want the mixture to be thick, so it sticks to the back of a spoon.

4. Stir in the peppermint extract.

5. Pour the thickened mixture into a medium bowl and refrigerate to cool.

6. Remove the metal bowl and the mixer beaters from the freezer. Pour the remaining 5 tablespoons of cream into the bowl. With the electric beater, whip the cream until it is thick and fluffy and forms peaks. Don't overbeat, or the cream will turn to butter.

7. Fold the cooled peppermint mixture into the whipped cream and stir in the chocolate chips.

8. Transfer to a freezer-safe container and freeze for at least 4 hours before serving.

## Tips

- Chilling the bowl and beaters in the freezer helps the cream whip faster and achieve better peaks
- Be careful not to overbeat the cream or it will turn to butter - stop as soon as peaks form
- The mixture should be thick enough to coat the back of a spoon before adding the peppermint extract
