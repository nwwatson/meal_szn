# Walnut Herb-Crusted Goat Cheese

A tart, creamy goat cheese log coated in a flavorful mixture of chopped walnuts and fresh herbs, perfect for keto and gluten-free diets

## Source
The Complete Ketogenic Guide for Beginners 

## Servings
4 servings

## Prep Time
10 minutes

## Cook Time
0 minutes

## Ingredients

- 6 ounces chopped walnuts
- 1 tablespoon chopped oregano
- 1 tablespoon chopped parsley
- 1 teaspoon chopped fresh thyme
- 1/4 teaspoon freshly ground black pepper
- 1 (8-ounce) log goat cheese

## Instructions

1. Place the walnuts, oregano, parsley, thyme, and pepper in a food processor and pulse until finely chopped.

2. Pour the walnut mixture onto a plate and roll the goat cheese log in the nut mixture, pressing so the cheese is covered and the walnut mixture sticks to the log.

3. Wrap the cheese in plastic and store in the refrigerator for up to 1 week.

4. Slice and enjoy!

## Tips

- Look for soft goat cheese rather than semihard or hard varieties, as the soft type contains zero carbs while harder varieties may contain carbohydrates.
- Press the walnut herb mixture firmly onto the cheese log to ensure good adhesion and even coating.
- This appetizer can be made up to a week ahead and stored wrapped in the refrigerator, making it perfect for meal prep or entertaining.
