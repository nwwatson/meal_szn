# Keto Blueberry Fat Bombs

A distinct blue-colored keto fat bomb made with coconut oil, cream cheese, and fresh blueberries. These frozen treats are gluten-free, nut-free, and vegetarian.

## Source
The Complete Ketogenic Guide for Beginners 

## Servings
12 fat bombs

## Prep Time
10 minutes

## Cook Time
0 minutes

## Ingredients

- 1/2 cup coconut oil, at room temperature
- 1/2 cup cream cheese, at room temperature
- 1/2 cup blueberries, mashed with a fork
- 6 drops liquid stevia
- Pinch ground nutmeg

## Instructions

1. Line a mini muffin tin with paper liners and set aside.

2. In a medium bowl, stir together the coconut oil and cream cheese until well blended.

3. Stir in the blueberries, stevia, and nutmeg until combined.

4. Divide the blueberry mixture into the muffin cups and place the tray in the freezer until set, about 3 hours.

5. Place the fat bombs in an airtight container and store in the freezer until you wish to eat them.

## Tips

- Use wild blueberries if available as they contain significantly higher levels of antioxidants than cultivated blueberries
- Frozen unsweetened berries work well if fresh are not available - just thaw them first before mashing
- Store in the freezer in an airtight container and eat directly from frozen for best texture
