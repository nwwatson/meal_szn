# Sautéed Asparagus with Walnuts

Tender asparagus spears sautéed until lightly browned and tossed with crunchy walnuts. A perfect keto-friendly side dish that's dairy-free, gluten-free, and vegetarian.

## Source
The Complete Ketogenic Guide for Beginners 

## Servings
4 servings

## Prep Time
10 minutes

## Cook Time
5 minutes

## Ingredients

- 1 1/2 tablespoons olive oil
- 3/4 pound asparagus, woody ends trimmed
- Sea salt to taste
- Freshly ground pepper to taste
- 1/4 cup chopped walnuts

## Instructions

1. Place a large skillet over medium-high heat and add the olive oil.

2. Sauté the asparagus until the spears are tender and lightly browned, about 5 minutes.

3. Season the asparagus with salt and pepper.

4. Remove the skillet from the heat and toss the asparagus with the walnuts.

5. Serve immediately.

## Tips

- Look for asparagus spears that are firm and bright green with tight tips for the best quality and flavor.
- To properly trim asparagus, bend each spear near the bottom and it will naturally break at the point where the woody end begins.
- Don't overcook the asparagus - it should be tender-crisp and bright green when done, which helps retain its nutritional value and prevents it from becoming mushy.
