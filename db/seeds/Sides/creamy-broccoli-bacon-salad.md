# Creamy Broccoli-Bacon Salad

This fresh, creamy cold salad is the perfect companion for grilled meats, fish, or any summer-friendly entrée. The crunchy raw broccoli is the star, but the honey mustard adds a nice sweet kick to the otherwise salty ingredients.

## Source
The Easy 5-Ingredient Ketogenic Diet Cookbook 

## Servings
2 servings

## Prep Time
10 minutes

## Cook Time
10 minutes

## Ingredients

- 6 bacon slices
- 1/2 pound fresh broccoli, cut into small florets
- 1/4 cup sliced almonds
- 1/3 cup mayonnaise
- 1 tablespoon honey mustard dressing

## Instructions

1. In a large skillet over medium-high heat, cook the bacon on both sides until crispy, about 8 minutes. Transfer the bacon to a paper towel-lined plate to drain and cool for 5 minutes. When cool, break the bacon into crumbles.

2. In a large bowl, combine the broccoli with the almonds and bacon.

3. In a small bowl, mix together the mayonnaise and honey mustard.

4. Add the dressing to the broccoli salad, and toss to thoroughly combine.

5. Chill the salad for 1 hour or more before serving.

## Tips

- You can replace the sliced almonds with sunflower seeds for a different crunch.
- Consider adding 1/2 red onion chopped, or 2 scallions chopped, or 2 carrots shredded for extra crispness and sweetness.
- Chill the salad for at least 1 hour before serving to allow the flavors to meld together.
