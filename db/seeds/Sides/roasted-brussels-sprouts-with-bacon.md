# Roasted Brussels Sprouts with Bacon

A simple one-pan dish where Brussels sprouts roast alongside bacon pieces for a flavorful, crispy side dish topped with Parmesan cheese

## Source
The Easy 5-Ingredient Ketogenic Diet Cookbook 

## Servings
2 servings

## Prep Time
5 minutes

## Cook Time
25 minutes

## Ingredients

- 1/2 pound Brussels sprouts, cleaned, trimmed, and halved
- 1 tablespoon olive oil
- Pink Himalayan salt to taste
- Freshly ground black pepper to taste
- 1 teaspoon red pepper flakes
- 6 bacon slices
- 1 tablespoon grated Parmesan cheese

## Instructions

1. Preheat the oven to 400°F.

2. In a medium bowl, toss the Brussels sprouts with the olive oil, season with pink Himalayan salt and pepper, and add the red pepper flakes.

3. Cut the bacon strips into 1-inch pieces using kitchen shears or a knife.

4. Place the Brussels sprouts and bacon on a baking sheet in a single layer. Roast for about 25 minutes. About halfway through the baking time, give the pan a little shake to move the sprouts around, or give them a stir. You want your Brussels sprouts crispy and browned on the outside.

5. Remove the Brussels sprouts from the oven. Divide them between two plates, top each serving with Parmesan cheese, and serve.

## Tips

- Leave out the Parmesan cheese to make this dish dairy-free
- Use kitchen shears to easily cut bacon into uniform pieces
- Make sure Brussels sprouts are in a single layer on the baking sheet for even browning and crispiness
