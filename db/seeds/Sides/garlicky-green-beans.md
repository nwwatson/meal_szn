# Garlicky Green Beans

Sizzling lightly caramelized green beans flavored generously with garlic are a perfect culinary storm of texture, color, and taste.

## Source
The Complete Ketogenic Guide for Beginners 

## Servings
4 servings

## Prep Time
10 minutes

## Cook Time
10 minutes

## Ingredients

- 1 pound green beans, stemmed
- 2 tablespoons olive oil
- 1 teaspoon minced garlic
- Freshly ground black pepper
- 1/4 cup freshly grated Parmesan cheese
- Sea salt

## Instructions

1. Preheat the oven to 425°F. Line a baking sheet with aluminum foil and set aside.

2. In a large bowl, toss together the green beans, olive oil, and garlic until well mixed.

3. Season the beans lightly with salt and pepper.

4. Spread the beans on the baking sheet and roast them until they are tender and lightly browned, stirring them once, about 10 minutes.

5. Serve topped with the Parmesan cheese.

## Tips

- Yellow wax beans or a combination of green and yellow beans would also be a lovely choice if you want to vary the ingredients.
- These beans are so delicious you might find yourself making them as a snack instead of just a side dish.
- Pairs well with quick-cooking entrees like herb butter scallops or pan-seared fish for a meal ready in under 30 minutes.
