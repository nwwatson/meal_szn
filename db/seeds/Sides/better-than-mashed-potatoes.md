# Better-Than-Mashed-Potatoes

A creamy, low-carb cauliflower mash that tastes better than traditional mashed potatoes

## Source
How Not To Get Sick

## Servings
4 servings

## Prep Time
10 minutes

## Cook Time
15 minutes

## Ingredients

- 1 head cauliflower, cut into florets
- 2 tablespoons unsalted butter, plus more to serve (optional)
- 2 teaspoons minced garlic
- 2 tablespoons cream cheese
- 2 tablespoons sour cream
- 1/2 teaspoon salt
- 1 tablespoon minced fresh chives

## Instructions

1. Pour an inch or so of water into a medium pot and add a steamer basket.

2. Bring the water to a boil over medium-high heat and add the cauliflower.

3. Cover and steam until soft, about 10 minutes. Transfer the cauliflower to a food processor.

4. Melt the butter in a small skillet over medium heat. Add the garlic and cook until fragrant, about 2 minutes.

5. Add the garlic and butter drippings to the food processor, along with the cream cheese, sour cream, and salt.

6. Blend until smooth. Top with additional butter, if you like, and chives.

## Tips

- Make sure to steam the cauliflower until very soft to achieve the smoothest, most potato-like texture
- Drain any excess water from the steamed cauliflower before processing to prevent a watery consistency
- For extra richness, add the optional butter on top when serving and garnish with fresh chives for color and flavor
