# The Best Broccoli

Perfectly roasted broccoli with garlic, lemon juice, and optional Parmesan cheese

## Source
How Not To Get Sick

## Servings
4 servings

## Prep Time
10 minutes

## Cook Time
15 minutes

## Ingredients

- Olive oil spray
- 3 heads broccoli
- 1/4 cup avocado oil
- 1 teaspoon minced garlic
- Salt and black pepper to taste
- 1/2 teaspoon lemon juice
- Grated Parmesan cheese, for garnish (optional)

## Instructions

1. Preheat the oven to 450 degrees F. Lightly coat 2 rimmed baking sheets with olive oil spray.

2. Cut the broccoli into florets, then cut each floret in half lengthwise to create flat sides.

3. Put the florets in a large bowl and toss with the avocado oil and garlic. Season with salt and pepper.

4. Spread the florets evenly over the prepared baking sheets.

5. Roast for 10-15 minutes, until tender and charred.

6. Sprinkle with the lemon juice and Parmesan, if desired, then toss and serve.

## Tips

- Cutting the florets in half lengthwise creates flat surfaces that caramelize beautifully in the oven
- Don't overcrowd the baking sheets - use two sheets to ensure proper roasting and charring
- Watch closely during the last few minutes of cooking to achieve the perfect balance of tender and charred
