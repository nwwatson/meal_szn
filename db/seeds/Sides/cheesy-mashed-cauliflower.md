# Cheesy Mashed Cauliflower

A lower-carb alternative to mashed potatoes with cheese, cream, and butter that creates a creamy, flavorful side dish perfect for keto diets

## Source
The Complete Ketogenic Guide for Beginners 

## Servings
4 servings

## Prep Time
15 minutes

## Cook Time
5 minutes

## Ingredients

- 1 head cauliflower, chopped roughly
- 1/2 cup shredded Cheddar cheese
- 1/4 cup heavy whipping cream
- 2 tablespoons butter, at room temperature
- Sea salt to taste
- Freshly ground black pepper to taste

## Instructions

1. Place a large saucepan filled three-quarters full with water over high heat and bring to a boil.

2. Blanch the cauliflower until tender, about 5 minutes, and drain.

3. Transfer the cauliflower to a food processor and add the cheese, heavy cream, and butter. Puree until very creamy and whipped.

4. Season with salt and pepper.

5. Serve immediately.

## Tips

- Make sure to drain the cauliflower thoroughly after blanching to prevent watery mashed cauliflower.
- Try adding mashed roasted garlic for a truly sublime variation on this side dish.
- Pairs excellently with dishes that have flavorful sauces like braised short ribs or coconut chicken.
