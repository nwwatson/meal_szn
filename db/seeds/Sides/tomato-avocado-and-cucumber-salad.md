# Tomato, Avocado, and Cucumber Salad

A flavorful salad that can be assembled in minutes, perfect for potlucks with crispy Persian cucumbers, fresh tomatoes, creamy avocado, and tangy feta cheese

## Source
The Easy 5-Ingredient Ketogenic Diet Cookbook 

## Servings
2 servings

## Prep Time
5 minutes

## Cook Time
0 minutes

## Ingredients

- 1/2 cup grape tomatoes, halved
- 4 small Persian cucumbers or 1 English cucumber, peeled and finely chopped
- 1 avocado, finely chopped
- 1/4 cup crumbled feta cheese
- 2 tablespoons vinaigrette salad dressing
- Pink Himalayan salt to taste
- Freshly ground black pepper to taste

## Instructions

1. In a large bowl, combine the tomatoes, cucumbers, avocado, and feta cheese.

2. Add the vinaigrette, and season with pink Himalayan salt and pepper. Toss to thoroughly combine.

3. Divide the salad between two plates and serve.

## Tips

- Use small Persian cucumbers for the crispiest texture and tiniest seeds
- You can substitute goat cheese for feta cheese if preferred
- Add finely chopped red onion or sliced black olives for extra flavor and texture
