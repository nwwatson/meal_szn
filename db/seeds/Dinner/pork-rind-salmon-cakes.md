# Pork Rind Salmon Cakes

Easy-to-make fish cakes using canned salmon and crushed pork rinds that act like a flavorful bread-crumb crust to help hold the cakes together

## Source
The Easy 5-Ingredient Ketogenic Diet Cookbook 

## Servings
2 servings

## Prep Time
10 minutes

## Cook Time
10 minutes

## Ingredients

- 6 ounces canned Alaska wild salmon, drained
- 2 tablespoons crushed pork rinds
- 1 egg, lightly beaten
- 3 tablespoons mayonnaise, divided
- Pink Himalayan salt to taste
- Freshly ground black pepper to taste
- 1 tablespoon ghee
- 1/2 tablespoon Dijon mustard

## Instructions

1. In a medium bowl, mix to combine the salmon, pork rinds, egg, and 1 1/2 tablespoons of mayonnaise, and season with pink Himalayan salt and pepper.

2. With the salmon mixture, form patties the size of hockey pucks or smaller. Keep patting the patties until they hold together.

3. In a medium skillet over medium-high heat, melt the ghee. When the ghee sizzles, place the salmon patties in the pan. Cook for about 3 minutes per side, until browned. Transfer the patties to a paper towel-lined plate.

4. In a small bowl, mix together the remaining 1 1/2 tablespoons of mayonnaise and the mustard.

5. Serve the salmon cakes with the mayo-mustard dipping sauce.

## Tips

- Choose wild-caught salmon over farmed salmon for better quality and taste - if labeled 'Atlantic salmon' it's typically farmed, so read labels carefully
- Wild Planet's canned salmon is recommended for its fresh-caught wild pink salmon quality and amazing taste
- Keep patting the salmon patties until they hold together well to prevent them from falling apart during cooking
