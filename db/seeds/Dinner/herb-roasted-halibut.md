# Herb-Roasted Halibut

Simple and flavorful roasted halibut fillets with herbs, garlic, and lemon

## Source
How Not To Get Sick

## Servings
5 servings

## Prep Time
10 minutes

## Cook Time
20 minutes

## Ingredients

- 5 (6-ounce) skin-on halibut fillets
- 1 tablespoon avocado oil
- 2 teaspoons minced garlic
- 1 teaspoon grated lemon zest
- 2 teaspoons dried marjoram
- Salt and black pepper to taste
- 1 lemon, cut into 5 thin slices

## Instructions

1. Preheat the oven to 425 degrees F. Lightly grease a baking pan.

2. Place the fillets in the prepared pan and drizzle with the avocado oil.

3. Sprinkle each fillet with the minced garlic, lemon zest, and marjoram and season with salt and pepper.

4. Top each fillet with a slice of lemon.

5. Roast for 15-20 minutes, until flaky and cooked through. Cover with aluminum foil if tops are getting too dark before the fillets are cooked through.

## Tips

- This recipe is also great with skin-on salmon fillets!
- Use a meat thermometer to ensure the fish reaches an internal temperature of 145°F
- Don't overcook the halibut as it can become dry - it should flake easily with a fork when done
