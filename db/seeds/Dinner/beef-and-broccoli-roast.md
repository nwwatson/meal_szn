# Beef and Broccoli Roast

A keto-friendly slow cooker meal with tender beef chuck roast, crisp broccoli, and a savory Asian-inspired sauce made with just 5 ingredients.

## Source
The Easy 5-Ingredient Ketogenic Diet Cookbook 

## Servings
2 servings

## Prep Time
10 minutes

## Cook Time
4 hours 30 minutes

## Ingredients

- 1 pound beef chuck roast
- Pink Himalayan salt to taste
- Freshly ground black pepper to taste
- 1/2 cup beef broth, plus more if needed
- 1/4 cup soy sauce (or coconut aminos)
- 1 teaspoon toasted sesame oil
- 1 (16-ounce) bag frozen broccoli

## Instructions

1. With the crock insert in place, preheat the slow cooker to low.

2. On a cutting board, season the chuck roast with pink Himalayan salt and pepper, and slice the roast thin. Put the sliced beef in the slow cooker.

3. In a small bowl, mix together the beef broth, soy sauce, and sesame oil. Pour over the beef.

4. Cover and cook on low for 4 hours.

5. Add the frozen broccoli, and cook for 30 minutes more. If you need more liquid, add additional beef broth.

6. Serve hot.

## Tips

- Serve over shirataki rice or cauliflower rice for a complete meal
- Slice the chuck roast thin for more tender results and better sauce absorption
- Keep extra beef broth on hand to add moisture if needed during cooking
