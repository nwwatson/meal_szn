# Glazed Sweet Onion Meatloaf

A low-carb meatloaf made with crushed pork rinds instead of breadcrumbs, topped with a sugar-free glaze

## Source
How Not To Get Sick

## Servings
6 servings

## Prep Time
15 minutes

## Cook Time
1 hour

## Ingredients

### Meatloaf
- 1 tablespoon olive oil
- 1 yellow onion, diced
- 1 tablespoon minced garlic
- 2 pounds ground beef
- 2 large eggs
- 2/3 cup crushed pork rinds
- 1/4 cup whole milk
- 1/4 cup no-added-sugar ketchup
- 2 tablespoons minced fresh parsley
- 2 teaspoons salt
- 1 teaspoon black pepper
- 1 teaspoon Italian seasoning
- 1/2 teaspoon paprika

### Glaze
- 3/4 cup no-sugar-added ketchup
- 2 tablespoons brown Swerve
- 2 teaspoons apple cider vinegar
- 1 teaspoon garlic powder
- 1/2 teaspoon onion powder
- 1/4 teaspoon salt
- 1/4 teaspoon black pepper

## Instructions

1. Preheat the oven to 375 degrees F. Line a 9 x 5-inch loaf pan with parchment paper.

2. In a small skillet, heat the olive oil over low heat. Add the onion and garlic and cook until translucent but not browned, about 3 minutes. Transfer to a large bowl and set aside to cool.

3. Add the remaining meatloaf ingredients to the bowl and mix well. Press the mixture into the prepared loaf pan.

4. Bake for 40 minutes.

5. In a small bowl, combine all of the glaze ingredients and mix well. Spread the glaze over the meatloaf and bake for another 15 minutes, or until the internal temperature reaches 160 degrees F.

6. Let rest for 10 minutes before slicing.

## Tips

- Use a meat thermometer to ensure the internal temperature reaches exactly 160°F for food safety without overcooking
- Let the meatloaf rest for the full 10 minutes before slicing to prevent it from falling apart
- Crush your own pork rinds in a food processor for the freshest texture and best binding results
