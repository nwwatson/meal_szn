# Creamy Slow-Cooker Chicken

A creamy slow cooker meal perfect for chilly days with beautiful fresh spinach added at the end for color and freshness

## Source
The Easy 5-Ingredient Ketogenic Diet Cookbook 

## Servings
2 servings

## Prep Time
10 minutes

## Cook Time
4 hours 15 minutes

## Ingredients

- 1 tablespoon ghee
- 2 boneless skinless chicken breasts
- 1 cup Alfredo sauce
- 1/4 cup chopped sun-dried tomatoes
- 1/4 cup grated Parmesan cheese
- Pink Himalayan salt to taste
- Freshly ground black pepper to taste
- 2 cups fresh spinach

## Instructions

1. In a medium skillet over medium-high heat, melt the ghee. Add the chicken and cook about 4 minutes on each side until brown.

2. With the crock insert in place, transfer the chicken to the slow cooker. Set the slow cooker to low.

3. In a small bowl, mix to combine the Alfredo sauce, sun-dried tomatoes, and Parmesan cheese, and season with pink Himalayan salt and pepper. Pour the sauce over the chicken.

4. Cover and cook on low for 4 hours, or until the chicken is cooked through.

5. Add the fresh spinach. Cover and cook for 5 minutes more until the spinach is slightly wilted, and serve.

## Tips

- You can replace the chicken with pork chops and follow the same instructions for variety
- Browning the chicken first adds extra flavor and helps seal in the juices
- Add the spinach only at the very end to maintain its bright color and prevent overcooking
