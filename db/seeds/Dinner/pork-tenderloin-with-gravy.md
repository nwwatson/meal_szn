# Pork Tenderloin with Gravy

Tender slow-cooked pork tenderloin with a savory gravy made from the braising liquid

## Source
How Not To Get Sick

## Servings
4 servings

## Prep Time
10 minutes

## Cook Time
4 hours

## Ingredients

### Pork
- 1 tablespoon olive oil
- 2 (1 1/4-pound) pork tenderloins
- 1 tablespoon cornstarch or agar powder
- 2 tablespoons water

### Marinade
- 1/2 cup chicken, beef, or vegetable broth
- 1/3 cup coconut aminos or soy sauce
- 3 tablespoons Worcestershire sauce
- 2 tablespoons lemon juice
- 1 tablespoon brown Swerve
- 1 tablespoon dried basil
- 2 teaspoons onion powder
- 2 teaspoons garlic powder
- 1 teaspoon minced garlic
- 1/2 teaspoon white pepper
- 1/4 teaspoon cayenne pepper

## Instructions

1. Heat the oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat. Add the pork tenderloins and sear on each side until golden, about 6 minutes. Transfer the tenderloins to a slow cooker.

2. In a medium bowl, combine all of the marinade ingredients and mix well. Pour the marinade over the tenderloins.

3. Cover and cook on high for 4 hours, or until the internal temperature reaches 150-160 degrees F.

4. Transfer the braising liquid to a skillet and heat over medium-low heat.

5. In a small bowl, whisk together the cornstarch and water, then whisk the mixture into the braising liquid.

6. Simmer until thickened, about 5 minutes. Serve the tenderloins with the gravy.

## Tips

- This tenderloin is also wonderful grilled without gravy. Marinate the tenderloin in half of the marinade for 30 minutes, reserving the other half, then grill covered for 15-20 minutes, turning every 5 minutes and brushing with reserved marinade.
- Use a meat thermometer to ensure the internal temperature reaches 150-160°F for perfectly cooked, juicy pork.
- Let the pork rest for 5 minutes before slicing to retain juices and ensure tender results.
