# Avocado Caprese Salad

A keto twist on the classic Caprese salad with added avocado for healthy fats and arugula for a peppery flavor that makes the dish super filling.

## Source
The Easy 5-Ingredient Ketogenic Diet Cookbook 

## Servings
2 servings

## Prep Time
5 minutes

## Cook Time
0 minutes

## Ingredients

- 2 cups arugula
- 1 tablespoon olive oil, divided
- Pink Himalayan salt to taste
- Freshly ground black pepper to taste
- 1 avocado, sliced
- 4 fresh mozzarella balls, sliced
- 1 Roma tomato, sliced
- 4 fresh basil leaves, cut into ribbons

## Instructions

1. In a large bowl, toss the arugula with 1/2 tablespoon of olive oil and season with pink Himalayan salt and pepper.

2. Divide the arugula between two plates.

3. Top the arugula with the avocado, mozzarella, and tomatoes, and drizzle with the remaining 1/2 tablespoon of olive oil. Season with pink Himalayan salt and pepper.

4. Sprinkle the basil on top and serve.

## Tips

- For an extra kick of flavor, replace the olive oil with a vinaigrette dressing like Primal Kitchen Greek Vinaigrette.
- Use fresh, high-quality mozzarella balls for the best texture and flavor.
- Choose a ripe but firm avocado to prevent it from becoming mushy when sliced.
