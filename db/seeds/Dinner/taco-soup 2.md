# Taco Soup

A creamy and rich soup with a nice kick of flavor from taco seasoning mixed with seasoned tomatoes. Perfect for dipping low-carb tortilla chips.

## Source
The Easy 5-Ingredient Ketogenic Diet Cookbook 

## Servings
4 servings

## Prep Time
5 minutes

## Cook Time
4 hours 10 minutes

## Ingredients

- 1 pound ground beef
- Pink Himalayan salt to taste
- Freshly ground black pepper to taste
- 2 cups beef broth
- 1 (10-ounce) can diced tomatoes
- 1 tablespoon taco seasoning
- 8 ounces cream cheese

## Instructions

1. With the crock insert in place, preheat the slow cooker to low.

2. On the stove top, in a medium skillet over medium-high heat, sauté the ground beef until browned, about 8 minutes, and season with pink Himalayan salt and pepper.

3. Add the ground beef, beef broth, tomatoes, taco seasoning, and cream cheese to the slow cooker.

4. Cover and cook on low for 4 hours, stirring occasionally.

5. Ladle into four bowls and serve.

## Tips

- Instead of ground beef, you can use spicy sausage for extra flavor.
- Make some low-carb tortilla chips to dip into this delicious mixture.
- Browning the ground beef on the stove top before adding to the slow cooker contributes extra flavor to the soup.
