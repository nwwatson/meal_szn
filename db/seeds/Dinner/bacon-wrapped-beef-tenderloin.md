# Bacon-Wrapped Beef Tenderloin

A throwback to a popular 1980s dish featuring lean beef tenderloin wrapped in bacon for salty richness and beautiful flavor combination

## Source
The Complete Ketogenic Guide for Beginners 

## Servings
4 servings

## Prep Time
10 minutes

## Cook Time
15 minutes

## Ingredients

- 4 (4-ounce) beef tenderloin steaks
- 8 bacon slices
- Sea salt
- Freshly ground black pepper
- 1 tablespoon extra-virgin olive oil

## Instructions

1. Preheat the oven to 450°F.

2. Season the steaks with salt and pepper.

3. Wrap each steak snugly around the edges with 2 slices of bacon and secure the bacon with toothpicks.

4. Place a large skillet over medium-high heat and add the olive oil.

5. Pan sear the steaks for 4 minutes per side and transfer them to a baking sheet.

6. Roast the steaks until they reach your desired doneness, about 6 minutes for medium.

7. Remove the steaks from the oven and let them rest for 10 minutes.

8. Remove the toothpicks and serve.

## Tips

- Source organic grass-fed beef for higher omega-3 fatty acids and vitamin E content
- Look for organic bacon with no additives, preferably from a reputable butcher to avoid high preservatives and sodium
- Let steaks rest for 10 minutes after cooking to allow juices to redistribute for optimal tenderness
