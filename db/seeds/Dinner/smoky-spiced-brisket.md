# Smoky Spiced Brisket

Tender slow-cooked beef brisket with a smoky spice rub and tangy barbecue sauce, finished in the oven for a caramelized exterior

## Source
How Not To Get Sick

## Servings
8 servings

## Prep Time
10 minutes

## Cook Time
510 minutes

## Ingredients

### Sauce
- 1 cup no-sugar-added ketchup
- 1/2 cup brown Swerve
- 1/3 cup apple cider vinegar
- 1 tablespoon minced garlic
- 1 tablespoon Worcestershire sauce
- 2 teaspoons black pepper
- 2 teaspoons ground mustard
- 2 teaspoons onion powder
- 1 teaspoon cayenne pepper
- 1 teaspoon liquid smoke

### Rub
- 1 tablespoon brown Swerve
- 1 tablespoon smoked paprika
- 1 teaspoon ground cumin
- 1 teaspoon onion powder
- 1 teaspoon garlic powder
- 1 teaspoon ground mustard
- 1 teaspoon dried oregano
- 1/2 teaspoon black pepper
- 1/2 teaspoon cayenne pepper
- 1 1/2 teaspoons salt

### Brisket
- 4 pounds beef brisket
- 2 tablespoons olive oil
- 1 tablespoon cornstarch or arrowroot powder
- 2 tablespoons water

## Instructions

1. Combine all of the sauce ingredients in a slow cooker and mix well.

2. In a small bowl, combine all of the rub ingredients. Rub the mixture all over the brisket.

3. Place the brisket in the slow cooker and spoon the sauce over it. Cover and cook on low for 8-10 hours, until the meat is tender and the internal temperature is 195 degrees F.

4. When the brisket is almost done, preheat the oven to 400 degrees F.

5. Transfer the brisket to a rimmed baking sheet. Drizzle with the olive oil and bake for 15 minutes to brown the top.

6. Meanwhile, transfer the sauce to a skillet. In a small bowl, whisk together the cornstarch and water, then whisk the mixture into the sauce in the skillet.

7. Cook the sauce over low heat until thickened, about 5 minutes.

8. Spread the sauce over the brisket and bake for another 10 minutes.

## Tips

- Use leftover brisket to make Kimchi and Brisket Stir-Fry by mixing sliced brisket and chopped kimchi into stir-fried rice.
- The brisket is done when it reaches an internal temperature of 195 degrees F and feels tender when pierced with a fork.
- Let the brisket rest for 10-15 minutes before slicing against the grain for maximum tenderness.
