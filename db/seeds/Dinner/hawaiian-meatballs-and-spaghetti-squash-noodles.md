# Hawaiian Meatballs and Spaghetti Squash Noodles

Flavorful meatballs made with ground beef and pork in a pineapple spaghetti sauce, served over baked spaghetti squash

## Source
How Not To Get Sick

## Servings
8 servings

## Prep Time
20 minutes

## Cook Time
1 hour 15 minutes

## Ingredients

### Meatballs
- 2 tablespoons olive oil, plus more for greasing
- 1 cup chopped yellow onion
- 1 teaspoon chopped garlic
- 1 tablespoon paprika
- 1 tablespoon five-spice powder
- 1 pound ground beef
- 1 pound ground pork
- 2 tablespoons almond flour
- 2 tablespoons grated Parmesan cheese, plus more for garnish
- 1 tablespoon minced fresh parsley, plus more for garnish
- 1 teaspoon salt

### Pineapple Spaghetti Sauce and Squash
- 1 tablespoon olive oil
- 1 cup sliced red onion
- 1 cup sliced green bell pepper
- 1/4 cup chopped fresh pineapple
- 1 (15-ounce) jar spaghetti sauce
- 2 spaghetti squash, cooked

## Instructions

1. Preheat the oven to 350 degrees F. Lightly grease a small baking pan with olive oil.

2. Heat 2 tablespoons olive oil in a small skillet over medium heat. Add the yellow onion, garlic, paprika, and five-spice and saute until the onion is soft.

3. Transfer the onion mixture to a large bowl and add the beef, pork, almond flour, Parmesan cheese, parsley, and salt. Mix until fully combined.

4. Form about 28 meatballs the size of golf balls and place in the prepared baking pan. Bake for 25 minutes, or until cooked through.

5. To make the sauce, heat 1 tablespoon olive oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat. Add the red onion and bell pepper and saute until soft, about 5 minutes.

6. Add the pineapple and saute for another 3 minutes. Add the spaghetti sauce and bring to a simmer.

7. Add the meatballs to the sauce and continue to simmer for another 10 minutes.

8. Serve over the baked spaghetti squash, using the squash halves as bowls. Garnish with additional parsley and Parmesan cheese.

## Tips

- If you serve the meatballs without the sauce and spaghetti squash, the dish drops down to 4% carbs
- Make sure to cook the spaghetti squash ahead of time for easier assembly
- Form uniform meatballs for even cooking - a small ice cream scoop can help with consistent sizing
