# Italian Beef Burgers

Juicy beef patties made with ground almonds, fresh basil, and garlic that can be topped with various ingredients like bacon, avocado, or tomato slices

## Source
The Complete Ketogenic Guide for Beginners 

## Servings
4 servings

## Prep Time
10 minutes

## Cook Time
12 minutes

## Ingredients

- 1 pound 75% lean ground beef
- 1/4 cup ground almonds
- 2 tablespoons chopped fresh basil
- 1 teaspoon minced garlic
- 1/4 teaspoon sea salt
- 1 tablespoon olive oil
- 1 tomato, cut into 4 thick slices
- 1/4 sweet onion, sliced thinly

## Instructions

1. In a medium bowl, mix together the ground beef, ground almonds, basil, garlic, and salt until well mixed.

2. Form the beef mixture into four equal patties and flatten them to about 1/2 inch thick.

3. Place a large skillet on medium-high heat and add the olive oil.

4. Pan-fry the burgers until cooked through, flipping them once, about 12 minutes in total.

5. Pat away any excess grease with paper towels and serve the burgers with a slice of tomato and onion.

## Tips

- Ground lamb can be substituted for ground beef, but choose 70% lean or less to maintain proper fat macros
- Don't make the patties too thick - 1/2 inch ensures even cooking and prevents dry burgers
- Top with creative combinations like bacon, avocado, homemade mayonnaise, or fresh tomato slices for added flavor and nutrition
