# Mississippi Pot Roast

A simple and flavorful slow cooker pot roast packed with pepperoncini and seasoned with ranch dressing and gravy packets

## Source
The Easy 5-Ingredient Ketogenic Diet Cookbook 

## Servings
8 servings

## Prep Time
5 minutes

## Cook Time
8 hours

## Ingredients

- 2-pound beef chuck roast
- Pink Himalayan salt to taste
- Freshly ground black pepper to taste
- 1/2 (1-ounce) packet dry Au Jus Gravy Mix
- 1/2 (1-ounce) packet dry ranch dressing
- 8 tablespoons butter (1 stick)
- 1 cup whole pepperoncini

## Instructions

1. With the crock insert in place, preheat the slow cooker to low.

2. Season both sides of the beef chuck roast with pink Himalayan salt and pepper. Put in the slow cooker.

3. Sprinkle the gravy mix and ranch dressing packets on top of the roast.

4. Place the butter on top of the roast, and sprinkle the pepperoncini around it.

5. Cover and cook on low for 8 hours.

6. Shred the beef using two forks, and serve hot.

## Tips

- You can make this recipe with boneless chicken breasts for a delicious variation
- Use Mezzetta brand pepperoncini for best results
- This recipe is perfect for meal prep as it reheats well throughout the week
