# Skirt Steak Cobb Salad

A flavorful ketogenic salad featuring quickly cooked skirt steak seasoned with pink Himalayan salt and black pepper, served over romaine lettuce with grape tomatoes, blue cheese, and pecans

## Source
The Easy 5-Ingredient Ketogenic Diet Cookbook 

## Servings
2 servings

## Prep Time
15 minutes

## Cook Time
10 minutes

## Ingredients

- 8 ounces skirt steak
- Pink Himalayan salt
- Freshly ground black pepper
- 1 tablespoon butter
- 2 romaine hearts or 2 cups chopped romaine
- 1/2 cup halved grape tomatoes
- 1/4 cup crumbled blue cheese
- 1/4 cup pecans
- 1 tablespoon olive oil

## Instructions

1. Heat a large skillet over high heat.

2. Pat the steak dry with a paper towel, and season both sides with pink Himalayan salt and pepper.

3. Add the butter to the skillet. When it melts, put the steak in the skillet.

4. Sear the steak for about 3 minutes on each side, for medium-rare.

5. Transfer the steak to a cutting board and let it rest for at least 5 minutes.

6. Meanwhile, divide the romaine between two plates, and top with the grape tomato halves, blue cheese, and pecans. Drizzle with the olive oil.

7. Slice the skirt steak across the grain, top the salads with it, and serve.

## Tips

- Cook the skirt steak just to medium-rare to keep it tender, and always cut it against the grain when serving.
- Use a screaming hot pan with oil for the perfect sear - no marinating needed, just salt and pepper.
- Flank steak works just as well as skirt steak as a substitution if preferred.
