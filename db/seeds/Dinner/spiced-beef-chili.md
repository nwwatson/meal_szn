# Spiced Beef Chili

A hearty and flavorful ground beef chili seasoned with warm spices including cinnamon, cocoa powder, and smoked paprika

## Source
How To Not Get Sick

## Servings
8 servings

## Prep Time
10 minutes

## Cook Time
1 hour 45 minutes

## Ingredients

- 1 tablespoon unsalted butter
- 2 tablespoons minced garlic
- 1 cup minced red bell pepper
- 1 white onion, chopped
- 2 pounds ground beef
- 1 tablespoon chili powder
- 1 tablespoon smoked paprika
- 1 tablespoon unsweetened cocoa powder
- 1/2 teaspoon ground allspice
- 1/4 teaspoon ground cloves
- 1 tablespoon ground cumin
- 1 tablespoon ground cinnamon
- 1 teaspoon salt
- 2 (14.5-ounce) cans diced tomatoes, undrained
- 1 tablespoon apple cider vinegar
- 1/2 cup beef broth
- 1 (4-ounce) can diced mild chiles, drained
- Sour cream, for topping (optional)
- Shredded cheddar cheese, for topping (optional)
- Sliced green onions, for topping (optional)

## Instructions

1. In a large pot, melt the butter over medium heat. Add the garlic, bell pepper, and onion and cook until tender, about 5 minutes.

2. Add the beef and cook, breaking it up, until no longer pink, about 10 minutes.

3. Add all of the remaining ingredients and stir well.

4. Bring to a simmer, then turn the heat to low and simmer for 1 1/2 hours.

5. Serve with your favorite toppings.

## Tips

- Make Chili-Stuffed Peppers by preheating oven to 400°F, cutting tops off 6 bell peppers, removing seeds and ribs, placing cut side up in baking pan, drizzling with avocado oil, filling with chili, topping with cheddar cheese, and baking for 30 minutes until bubbly.
- The cocoa powder and cinnamon add depth and warmth to the chili without making it sweet - don't skip these unique ingredients.
- Let the chili simmer low and slow for the full 1 1/2 hours to allow all the spices to meld together and create rich, complex flavors.
