# Lemon Butter Chicken

Juicy bone-in chicken thighs braised in a creamy lemon butter sauce

## Source
Recipe Book

## Servings
4 servings

## Prep Time
10 minutes

## Cook Time
40 minutes

## Ingredients

- 4 bone-in, skin-on chicken thighs
- Sea salt to taste
- Freshly ground black pepper to taste
- 2 tablespoons butter, divided
- 2 teaspoons minced garlic
- 1/2 cup Herbed Chicken Stock
- 1/2 cup heavy (whipping) cream
- Juice of 1/2 lemon

## Instructions

1. Preheat the oven to 400°F.

2. Lightly season the chicken thighs with salt and pepper.

3. Place a large ovenproof skillet over medium-high heat and add 1 tablespoon of butter.

4. Brown the chicken thighs until golden on both sides, about 6 minutes in total. Remove the thighs to a plate and set aside.

5. Add the remaining 1 tablespoon of butter and sauté the garlic until translucent, about 2 minutes.

6. Whisk in the chicken stock, heavy cream, and lemon juice.

7. Bring the sauce to a boil and then return the chicken to the skillet.

8. Place the skillet in the oven, covered, and braise until the chicken is cooked through, about 30 minutes.

## Tips

- Choose organic grass-fed chicken when possible as it contains more vitamin A and omega-3 fatty acids and has better flavor than factory-farmed chicken.
- Chicken thighs are more flavorful and juicier than breasts, and they're also more budget-friendly while being less likely to dry out during cooking.
- Make sure to use an ovenproof skillet since this recipe transitions from stovetop to oven cooking.
