# Cheesy Bacon and Broccoli Chicken

A ketogenic casserole with shredded chicken, cream cheese, broccoli, bacon, and cheddar cheese - perfect for using up leftovers or making fresh.

## Source
The Easy 5-Ingredient Ketogenic Diet Cookbook 

## Servings
2 servings

## Prep Time
10 minutes

## Cook Time
60 minutes

## Ingredients

- 2 tablespoons ghee
- 2 boneless skinless chicken breasts
- Pink Himalayan salt to taste
- Freshly ground black pepper to taste
- 4 bacon slices
- 6 ounces cream cheese, at room temperature
- 2 cups frozen broccoli florets, thawed
- 1/2 cup shredded cheddar cheese

## Instructions

1. Preheat the oven to 375°F.

2. Choose a baking dish that is large enough to hold both chicken breasts and coat it with the ghee.

3. Pat dry the chicken breasts with a paper towel, and season with pink Himalayan salt and pepper.

4. Place the chicken breasts and the bacon slices in the baking dish, and bake for 25 minutes.

5. Transfer the chicken to a cutting board and use two forks to shred it. Season it again with pink Himalayan salt and pepper.

6. Place the bacon on a paper towel-lined plate to crisp up, and then crumble it.

7. In a medium bowl, mix to combine the cream cheese, shredded chicken, broccoli, and half of the bacon crumbles. Transfer the chicken mixture to the baking dish, and top with the cheddar and the remaining half of the bacon crumbles.

8. Bake until the cheese is bubbling and browned, about 35 minutes, and serve.

## Tips

- Make sure cream cheese is at room temperature for easier mixing and smoother texture.
- You can substitute cauliflower for broccoli if you prefer a different vegetable.
- This recipe works great with leftover cooked chicken and bacon to save time - just skip the initial baking step and go straight to mixing the ingredients.
