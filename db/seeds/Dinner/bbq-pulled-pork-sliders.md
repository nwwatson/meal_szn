# BBQ Pulled Pork Sliders

Slow-cooked pork shoulder with spice rub, served as sliders with coleslaw and optional toppings

## Source
How Not To Get Sick

## Servings
12 servings

## Prep Time
15 minutes

## Cook Time
8 hours

## Ingredients

### Pork Roast
- 2 tablespoons brown Swerve
- 2 tablespoons smoked paprika
- 1 tablespoon dried oregano
- 1 tablespoon ground cumin
- 2 teaspoons salt
- 1 teaspoon black pepper
- 1 (5-pound) pork shoulder roast
- 1 white onion, chopped
- 1 tablespoon minced garlic
- 1 cup bone broth
- No-sugar-added barbecue sauce (optional)

### Coleslaw
- 1/4 cup mayonnaise
- 2 tablespoons apple cider vinegar
- 2 tablespoons granular Swerve
- 1/2 teaspoon celery seed
- 1/4 teaspoon salt
- Pinch white pepper
- 4 cups shredded cabbage or packaged coleslaw mix

### Sliders
- 12 soft dinner rolls (optional)
- No-sugar-added pickle chips, chopped (optional)
- Shredded mozzarella (optional)

## Instructions

1. In a small bowl, mix together the Swerve, smoked paprika, oregano, cumin, salt, and black pepper. Rub the spice blend all over the roast.

2. Place the roast in a slow cooker. Top with the onion, garlic, and broth. Cover and cook on high for 8 hours.

3. Remove the roast from the slow cooker and shred. Season with salt and the BBQ sauce if desired.

4. In a large bowl, mix together the mayonnaise, vinegar, Swerve, celery seed, salt, and white pepper. Add the shredded cabbage and toss to coat.

5. Serve the pulled pork on the rolls, topped with coleslaw, pickles, mozzarella, and additional barbecue sauce if desired.

## Tips

- Sliders are super fun, but you can also serve this tasty BBQ pulled pork in a bowl topped with the coleslaw
- For extra flavor, let the spice-rubbed pork sit in the refrigerator for a few hours or overnight before cooking
- Save some of the cooking liquid from the slow cooker to moisten the pulled pork if it becomes dry during storage
