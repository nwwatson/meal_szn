# Cheeseburger Salad

A lettuce-wrapped burger taste in salad form, mixed in one bowl with pickles for extra flavor

## Source
The Easy 5-Ingredient Ketogenic Diet Cookbook 

## Servings
2 servings

## Prep Time
10 minutes

## Cook Time
10 minutes

## Ingredients

- 1 tablespoon ghee
- 1 pound ground beef
- Pink Himalayan salt to taste
- Freshly ground black pepper to taste
- 1/2 cup finely chopped dill pickles
- 2 cups chopped romaine lettuce
- 1/2 cup shredded Cheddar cheese
- 2 tablespoons ranch salad dressing

## Instructions

1. In a medium skillet over medium-high heat, heat the ghee.

2. When the ghee is hot, add the ground beef, breaking it up into smaller pieces with a spoon. Stir, cooking until the beef is browned, about 10 minutes. Season with pink Himalayan salt and pepper.

3. Put the pickles in a large bowl, and add the romaine and cheese.

4. Using a slotted spoon, transfer the browned beef from the skillet to the bowl.

5. Top the salad with the dressing, and toss to thoroughly coat.

6. Divide into two bowls and serve.

## Tips

- You can substitute ground turkey for the ground beef for a leaner option
- Add extra pickles if you love them - they really enhance the cheeseburger flavor
- For extra flavor, add diced onion to the salad and mix yellow mustard and paprika into the ranch dressing
