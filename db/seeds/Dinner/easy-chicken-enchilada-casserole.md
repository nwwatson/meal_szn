# Easy Chicken Enchilada Casserole

A low-carb chicken enchilada casserole with cream cheese, green chiles, and Mexican cheese blend

## Source
How Not To Get Sick

## Servings
6 servings

## Prep Time
15 minutes

## Cook Time
35 minutes

## Ingredients

- 1 tablespoon olive oil
- 1 teaspoon minced garlic
- 1 yellow onion, diced
- 1 (4-ounce) can diced mild green chiles, drained
- 4 ounces cream cheese
- 8 cups shredded cooked chicken (about 2 pounds)
- 1/4 cup minced fresh cilantro, plus more for garnish (optional)
- 1 tablespoon fajita seasoning
- 1 teaspoon salt
- 1/2 teaspoon black pepper
- 1 (14-ounce) can enchilada sauce
- 1 cup shredded Mexican-style four-cheese blend
- Sour cream, for garnish (optional)
- Cherry tomatoes, chopped, for garnish (optional)
- Lime wedges, for garnish (optional)

## Instructions

1. Preheat the oven to 350 degrees F.

2. Heat the oil in a medium skillet over medium-high heat. Add the garlic and cook until fragrant, about 2 minutes. Add the onion and cook until soft, about 3 minutes. Stir in the chiles.

3. Stir in the cream cheese until melted, then remove the pan from the heat.

4. Put the shredded chicken in a large bowl. Stir in the cilantro, fajita seasoning, salt, and pepper. Add the cream cheese mixture and mix until fully combined.

5. Spread 1/4 cup of enchilada sauce in the bottom of a 9 x 13-inch baking pan. Add the chicken mixture.

6. Top with the remaining sauce and sprinkle with the shredded cheese. Bake for 20-25 minutes, until the cheese is melted.

7. Serve topped with sour cream, cherry tomatoes, more cilantro, and lime wedges, if desired.

## Tips

- For a traditional enchilada version, roll the chicken filling in egg tortillas, place seam side down in the baking pan, and top with sauce and cheese before baking.
- Use rotisserie chicken to save time on prep - just shred it and add to the recipe.
- Let the casserole rest for 5-10 minutes after baking to help it set before serving.
