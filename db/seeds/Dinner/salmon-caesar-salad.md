# Salmon Caesar Salad

A creamy salad full of healthy fats with the salty crunch of bacon, featuring salmon and avocado instead of traditional carb-heavy croutons

## Source
The Easy 5-Ingredient Ketogenic Diet Cookbook 

## Servings
2 servings

## Prep Time
5 minutes

## Cook Time
20 minutes

## Ingredients

- 4 bacon slices
- 2 (6-ounce) salmon fillets
- Pink Himalayan salt to taste
- Freshly ground black pepper to taste
- 1 tablespoon ghee, if needed
- 1/2 avocado, sliced
- 2 romaine hearts or 2 cups chopped romaine
- 2 tablespoons Caesar dressing (Primal Kitchen Caesar with Avocado Oil recommended)

## Instructions

1. In a medium skillet over medium-high heat, cook the bacon on both sides until crispy, about 8 minutes. Transfer the bacon to a paper towel-lined plate.

2. Meanwhile, pat the salmon with a paper towel to remove excess water. Season both sides with pink Himalayan salt and pepper.

3. With the bacon grease still in the skillet, add the salmon. If you need more grease in the pan, add the ghee to the bacon grease.

4. Cook the salmon for 5 minutes on each side, or until it reaches your preferred degree of doneness.

5. Tear the bacon into pieces. Season the avocado with pink Himalayan salt and pepper.

6. Divide the romaine, bacon, and avocado between two plates.

7. Top the salads with the salmon fillets, drizzle Caesar dressing on top, and serve.

## Tips

- For oven preparation: Preheat oven to 400°F and line a baking sheet with foil. Place bacon and salmon on the sheet, bake for 15 minutes, then broil for 5 minutes until salmon browns on top.
- Cook salmon to medium-rare for optimal texture and flavor - the internal temperature should reach 125°F.
- Save the bacon grease for cooking the salmon as it adds extra flavor and keeps the dish in one pan.
