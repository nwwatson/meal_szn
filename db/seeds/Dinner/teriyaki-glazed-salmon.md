# Teriyaki Glazed Salmon

Tender salmon fillets glazed with a homemade teriyaki sauce, baked to perfection

## Source
How Not To Get Sick

## Servings
4 servings

## Prep Time
20 minutes

## Cook Time
25 minutes

## Ingredients

### Salmon
- 1/4 cup salt
- 2 tablespoons granular Swerve
- 8 cups water
- 4 (8-ounce) skin-on salmon fillets
- 2 tablespoons avocado oil
- Fresh thyme, for garnish (optional)

### Teriyaki Glaze
- 1 teaspoon cornstarch or agar powder
- 1 tablespoon water
- 1/4 cup soy sauce or coconut aminos
- 2 tablespoons lemon juice
- 2 tablespoons brown Swerve
- 1 tablespoon minced garlic
- 1 teaspoon salt

## Instructions

1. Preheat the oven to 300 degrees F.

2. In a large bowl, dissolve the salt and Swerve in the water. Add the fillets and set aside at room temperature for 15 minutes.

3. Remove the fillets and pat dry with paper towels.

4. To make the glaze, in a medium bowl, whisk together the cornstarch and water. Add the soy sauce, lemon juice, Swerve, garlic, and salt and mix well.

5. Heat the oil in a large oven-safe skillet over medium-high heat. Add the fillets, skin side up, and cook until the flesh is browned, 2-3 minutes.

6. Flip the fillets over and cover with the glaze.

7. Transfer the skillet to the oven and bake for 15-20 minutes, until flaky and cooked through.

8. Garnish with thyme, if desired.

## Tips

- Brining the salmon for 15 minutes helps remove excess moisture and ensures better browning and flavor
- Make sure to use an oven-safe skillet for the stovetop-to-oven cooking method
- Check doneness by gently flaking the fish with a fork - it should separate easily when fully cooked
