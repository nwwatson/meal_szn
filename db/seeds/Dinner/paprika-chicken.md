# Paprika Chicken

Tender chicken breasts cooked in a creamy paprika sauce with sweet onions, heavy cream, and sour cream. The smoked paprika adds rich color and piquant flavor to this gluten-free, nut-free dish.

## Source
The Complete Ketogenic Guide for Beginners 

## Servings
4 servings

## Prep Time
10 minutes

## Cook Time
25 minutes

## Ingredients

- 4 (4-ounce) chicken breasts, skin-on
- Sea salt to taste
- Freshly ground black pepper to taste
- 1 tablespoon olive oil
- 1/2 cup chopped sweet onion
- 1/2 cup heavy (whipping) cream
- 2 teaspoons smoked paprika
- 1/2 cup sour cream
- 2 tablespoons chopped fresh parsley

## Instructions

1. Lightly season the chicken with salt and pepper.

2. Place a large skillet over medium-high heat and add the olive oil.

3. Sear the chicken on both sides until almost cooked through, about 15 minutes in total. Remove the chicken to a plate.

4. Add the onion to the skillet and saute until tender, about 4 minutes.

5. Stir in the cream and paprika and bring the liquid to a simmer.

6. Return the chicken and any accumulated juices to the skillet and simmer the chicken for 5 minutes until completely cooked.

7. Stir in the sour cream and remove the skillet from the heat.

8. Serve topped with the parsley.

## Tips

- Use smoked paprika for the best flavor, as it adds a deeper, more complex taste than regular sweet paprika.
- Don't let the sauce boil after adding the sour cream to prevent curdling - keep it at a gentle simmer.
- Let the chicken rest for a few minutes before serving to allow the juices to redistribute for maximum tenderness.
