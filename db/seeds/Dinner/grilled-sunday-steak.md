# Grilled Sunday Steak

Perfectly seasoned grilled filet mignon steaks with a simple spice rub

## Source
How Not To Get Sick

## Servings
4 servings

## Prep Time
20 minutes, plus 3 hours marinating

## Cook Time
10 minutes

## Ingredients

- 4 (6-ounce) filet mignons
- 1 teaspoon kosher salt
- 1/2 teaspoon black pepper
- 1/2 teaspoon paprika
- 1/2 teaspoon garlic powder

## Instructions

1. Pat the steaks dry with a paper towel, then sprinkle on both sides with the salt, pepper, paprika, and garlic powder.

2. Cover with aluminum foil and refrigerate for 3 hours, then set aside at room temperature for 20 minutes before grilling.

3. Preheat a grill to high heat.

4. For medium-rare steaks, sear for 1 1/2 minutes per side, then reduce the heat to medium and grill for another 1 1/2-2 minutes per side. If you prefer your steaks medium, add 1 more minute per side.

5. Let the steaks sit for 5 minutes before serving.

## Tips

- Bringing steaks to room temperature before grilling ensures even cooking throughout
- Use a meat thermometer for precision: 130-135°F for medium-rare, 135-145°F for medium
- Letting the steaks rest after cooking allows the juices to redistribute for maximum tenderness
