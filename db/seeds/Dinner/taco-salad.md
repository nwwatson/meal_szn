# Taco Salad

Perfect keto-friendly taco salad made without the shell, featuring browned ground beef combined with fresh ingredients

## Source
The Easy 5-Ingredient Ketogenic Diet Cookbook 

## Servings
2 servings

## Prep Time
10 minutes

## Cook Time
10 minutes

## Ingredients

- 1 tablespoon ghee
- 1 pound ground beef
- Pink Himalayan salt to taste
- Freshly ground black pepper to taste
- 2 cups chopped romaine lettuce
- 1 avocado, cubed
- 1/2 cup halved grape tomatoes
- 1/2 cup shredded Mexican blend cheese

## Instructions

1. In a large skillet over medium-high heat, heat the ghee.

2. When the ghee is hot, add the ground beef, breaking it up into smaller pieces with a spoon. Stir, cooking until the beef is browned, about 10 minutes. Season with pink Himalayan salt and pepper.

3. Divide the romaine into two bowls. Season with pink Himalayan salt and pepper.

4. Add the avocado and tomatoes, top with the beef and shredded cheese, and serve.

## Tips

- You could replace pink Himalayan salt and pepper with taco seasoning for extra flavor
- Feel free to add a dollop of sour cream and some chopped scallions or jalapeños for crunch and healthy fat
- You can make your own tortilla strips by cutting a low-carb tortilla into strips, tossing in olive oil, seasoning with salt and pepper, and baking at 425°F for 10 minutes
