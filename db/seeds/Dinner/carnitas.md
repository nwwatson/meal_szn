# Carnitas

Easy slow-cooked pork carnitas that are perfect for meal prep. The pork is cooked low and slow with onions and garlic to enhance the flavor.

## Source
The Easy 5-Ingredient Ketogenic Diet Cookbook 

## Servings
2 servings

## Prep Time
10 minutes

## Cook Time
8 hours

## Ingredients

- 1/2 tablespoon chili powder
- 1 tablespoon olive oil
- 1 pound boneless pork butt roast
- 2 garlic cloves, minced
- 1/2 small onion, diced
- Pinch pink Himalayan salt
- Pinch freshly ground black pepper
- Juice of 1 lime

## Instructions

1. With the crock insert in place, preheat the slow cooker to low.

2. In a small bowl, mix to combine the chili powder and olive oil, and rub it all over the pork.

3. Place the pork in the slow cooker, fat-side up.

4. Top the pork with the garlic, onion, pink Himalayan salt, pepper, and lime juice.

5. Cover and cook on low for 8 hours.

6. Transfer the pork to a cutting board, shred the meat with two forks, and serve.

## Tips

- Save the juices from the slow cooker after cooking the carnitas so you can drizzle them on top before serving.
- Use the reserved cooking juices when reheating leftover carnitas in a skillet for extra flavor.
- These carnitas are perfect for meal prep and can be used in various dishes throughout the week.
