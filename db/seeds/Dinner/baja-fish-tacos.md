# Baja Fish Tacos

Crispy cheese taco shells filled with seasoned tilapia and topped with creamy adobo sauce

## Source
How Not To Get Sick

## Servings
8 tacos

## Prep Time
15 minutes

## Cook Time
35 minutes

## Ingredients

### Taco Shells and Fish
- 2 cups shredded cheddar cheese
- 1 tablespoon ground cumin
- 1/2 teaspoon cayenne pepper
- 1/2 teaspoon salt
- Pinch black pepper
- 2 (6-ounce) tilapia fillets
- 2 teaspoons olive oil
- 2 teaspoons unsalted butter, cut in half

### Creamy Adobo Sauce
- 1/4 cup mayonnaise
- 1/2 cup sour cream
- 1/4 cup canned chipotles in adobo sauce
- 1 tablespoon lime juice
- 1 teaspoon garlic powder
- 1 teaspoon granular Swerve
- 1/4 teaspoon sea salt

### Optional Toppings
- Lime slices
- Chopped fresh cilantro
- Shredded red cabbage
- Minced red onion
- Crumbled cotija cheese

## Instructions

1. Preheat the oven to 350 degrees F. Line a rimmed baking sheet with parchment paper.

2. Place the shredded cheese in eight 1/4-cup mounds on the prepared baking sheet and flatten into circles. Bake for 5-7 minutes, until golden and browned on the edges.

3. Increase the oven temperature to 375 degrees F.

4. Prop a wooden spoon with a rounded handle between 2 mugs, and lay a sheet of parchment paper over the spoon handle. Working 1 or 2 at a time, drape the cheese circles on the parchment so that they hang down and take on a crispy tortilla shell shape.

5. Let cool for 5 minutes, until hardened, then transfer to a plate. Repeat with the remaining cheese circles.

6. Replace the parchment paper on the baking sheet. Place the fillets on the parchment.

7. In a small bowl, combine the cumin, cayenne, salt, and black pepper. Rub the spice mix into the fillets.

8. Drizzle the fillets with the olive oil and top each with 1 teaspoon of butter. Bake for 20-25 minutes, until flaky.

9. Meanwhile, combine all of the sauce ingredients in a blender or food processor and blend until smooth.

10. Turn on the broiler and broil the fillets for 3 minutes, until golden.

11. Divide the fillets between the taco shells and top with sauce and any of your favorite toppings.

## Tips

- Watch the cheese shells carefully while baking as they can go from golden to burnt quickly
- Make sure the cheese shells are completely cool before removing them from the parchment to prevent breaking
- The creamy adobo sauce can be made ahead of time and stored in the refrigerator for up to 3 days
