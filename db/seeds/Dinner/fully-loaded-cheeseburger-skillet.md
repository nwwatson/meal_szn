# Fully Loaded Cheeseburger Skillet

A low-carb skillet meal featuring seasoned ground beef topped with melted cheddar cheese and homemade Thousand Island dressing

## Source
How Not To Get Sick

## Servings
6 servings

## Prep Time
20 minutes

## Cook Time
15 minutes

## Ingredients

### Thousand Island Dressing
- 1/4 cup sour cream
- 3 tablespoons mayonnaise
- 2 tablespoons no-sugar-added ketchup
- 1 tablespoon minced pickles
- 1 teaspoon granular Swerve
- 1/2 teaspoon apple cider vinegar
- 1/4 teaspoon onion powder
- Pinch salt
- Pinch black pepper

### Beef
- 2 pounds ground beef
- 1/4 cup finely chopped white onion
- 2 tablespoons no-sugar-added ketchup
- 1 tablespoon yellow mustard
- 1 teaspoon garlic powder
- 1 teaspoon onion powder
- 1 teaspoon salt
- 1/2 teaspoon black pepper
- 1 1/2 cups shredded cheddar cheese, divided

### Optional Toppings
- Shredded iceberg lettuce
- Pickle slices
- Chopped red onion
- Chopped tomatoes

## Instructions

1. In a small bowl, whisk together all of the dressing ingredients until smooth; set aside.

2. In a large bowl, combine the ground beef, onion, ketchup, mustard, garlic powder, onion powder, salt, and pepper and mix well.

3. Heat a large skillet over medium-high heat and add the beef mixture. Cook, breaking it up, until no longer pink, about 12 minutes.

4. Drain off any excess grease and liquid. Stir in 3/4 cup of cheese.

5. Remove from the heat and top with the remaining 3/4 cup of cheese.

6. Drizzle with the dressing and serve with your favorite toppings.

## Tips

- Make the Thousand Island dressing ahead of time and store in the refrigerator for up to a week for quick meal prep
- Drain the beef thoroughly after cooking to prevent the skillet from becoming greasy and to help the cheese melt properly
- Serve over cauliflower rice or lettuce wraps for an even more complete low-carb cheeseburger experience
