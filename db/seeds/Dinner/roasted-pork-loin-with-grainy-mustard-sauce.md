# Roasted Pork Loin with Grainy Mustard Sauce

Tender roasted pork loin served with a rich and creamy grainy mustard sauce that's perfect for special occasions

## Source
The Complete Ketogenic Guide for Beginners 

## Servings
8 servings

## Prep Time
10 minutes

## Cook Time
70 minutes

## Ingredients

- 1 (2-pound) boneless pork loin roast
- Sea salt to taste
- Freshly ground black pepper to taste
- 3 tablespoons olive oil
- 1 1/2 cups heavy (whipping) cream
- 3 tablespoons grainy mustard, such as Pommery

## Instructions

1. Preheat the oven to 375°F.

2. Season the pork roast all over with sea salt and pepper.

3. Place a large skillet over medium-high heat and add the olive oil.

4. Brown the roast on all sides in the skillet, about 6 minutes in total, and place the roast in a baking dish.

5. Roast until a meat thermometer inserted in the thickest part of the roast reads 155°F, about 1 hour.

6. When there is approximately 15 minutes of roasting time left, place a small saucepan over medium heat and add the heavy cream and mustard.

7. Stir the sauce until it simmers, then reduce the heat to low. Simmer the sauce until it is very rich and thick, about 5 minutes. Remove the pan from the heat and set aside.

8. Let the pork rest for 10 minutes before slicing and serve with the sauce.

## Tips

- Look for Pommery mustard, which is slightly sweet and adds a lovely burst of flavor to this decadent sauce.
- Make sure to let the pork rest for the full 10 minutes after roasting to allow the juices to redistribute for maximum tenderness.
- Consider doubling the sauce recipe as it's delicious enough to eat by the spoonful and works great with other meats like beef tenderloin or lamb rack.
