# Baked Lemon-Butter Fish

Buttery, flaky fish with bright lemon and zesty capers that can be enjoyed any day of the week

## Source
The Easy 5-Ingredient Ketogenic Diet Cookbook 

## Servings
2 servings

## Prep Time
10 minutes

## Cook Time
20 minutes

## Ingredients

- 4 tablespoons butter, plus more for coating
- 2 (5-ounce) tilapia fillets
- Pink Himalayan salt to taste
- Freshly ground black pepper to taste
- 2 garlic cloves, minced
- 1 lemon, zested and juiced
- 2 tablespoons capers, rinsed and chopped

## Instructions

1. Preheat the oven to 400°F. Coat an 8-inch baking dish with butter.

2. Pat dry the tilapia with paper towels, and season on both sides with pink Himalayan salt and pepper. Place in the prepared baking dish.

3. In a medium skillet over medium heat, melt the butter. Add the garlic and cook for 3 to 5 minutes, until slightly browned but not burned.

4. Remove the garlic butter from the heat, and mix in the lemon zest and 2 tablespoons of lemon juice.

5. Pour the lemon-butter sauce over the fish, and sprinkle the capers around the baking pan.

6. Bake for 12 to 15 minutes, until the fish is just cooked through, and serve.

## Tips

- Pat the fish completely dry before seasoning to ensure proper browning and prevent excess moisture in the dish
- Watch the garlic carefully while cooking to prevent burning, which would make the sauce bitter
- Any mild white fish or even salmon can be substituted for the tilapia with delicious results
