# Mediterranean Turkey Bowls

A healthy Mediterranean-style bowl with seasoned ground turkey, fresh cucumber salad, and creamy yogurt sauce

## Source
How Not To Get Sick

## Servings
4 servings

## Prep Time
20 minutes

## Cook Time
15 minutes

## Ingredients

### Turkey
- 2 tablespoons olive oil
- 1 tablespoon minced garlic
- 1/2 cup diced yellow onion
- 1 pound ground turkey
- 1 tablespoon Italian seasoning
- 1/2 teaspoon salt
- 1/4 teaspoon black pepper
- Dash lemon juice

### Yogurt Sauce
- 1/3 cup grated cucumber, drained
- 1/2 cup plain full-fat Greek yogurt
- 2 teaspoons lemon juice
- 2 teaspoons olive oil
- 1/4 teaspoon grated garlic
- 1/8 teaspoon salt
- 1 tablespoon minced fresh dill

### Cucumber Salad
- 1 large cucumber, diced
- 1/4 cup halved pitted Kalamata olives
- 1 cup halved cherry tomatoes
- 1/4 cup crumbled feta cheese
- 1/4 cup minced red onion
- 1 tablespoon minced fresh parsley

### Dill Dressing
- 1/4 cup olive oil
- 2 tablespoons red wine vinegar
- 1 tablespoon minced fresh dill
- 1/2 teaspoon granular Swerve
- 1/2 teaspoon minced garlic
- 1/2 teaspoon dried Italian seasoning
- 1/8 teaspoon salt

### For Garnish
- 1/4 cup chopped fresh parsley
- 1/4 cup crumbled feta cheese
- 1 lemon, sliced (optional)
- 4 radishes, sliced (optional)

## Instructions

1. To make the turkey, heat the oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat. Add the garlic and onion and saute until soft, about 3 minutes.

2. Add the ground turkey and cook, breaking it up with a wooden spoon, until browned, about 8 minutes. Stir in the Italian seasoning, salt, pepper, and lemon juice.

3. To make the yogurt sauce, combine all the yogurt sauce ingredients in a medium bowl and stir until fully combined.

4. To make the cucumber salad, in a medium bowl combine the cucumber, olives, tomatoes, feta cheese, onion, and parsley.

5. In a small bowl, whisk together all the dressing ingredients and pour over the cucumber salad. Toss until well coated.

6. Divide the turkey mixture and cucumber salad evenly into 4 bowls.

7. Add a dollop of yogurt sauce to each bowl and garnish with the parsley, feta cheese, lemon slices, and radishes.

## Tips

- Make sure to drain the grated cucumber for the yogurt sauce well to prevent the sauce from becoming watery
- The turkey mixture and cucumber salad can be prepared ahead of time and stored separately in the refrigerator for up to 3 days
- For extra flavor, let the cucumber salad marinate with the dressing for 15-20 minutes before serving
