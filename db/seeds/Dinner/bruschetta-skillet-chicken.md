# Bruschetta Skillet Chicken

Tender chicken breasts topped with fresh bruschetta and Parmesan cheese, cooked in a single skillet

## Source
How Not To Get Sick

## Servings
4 servings

## Prep Time
20 minutes

## Cook Time
25 minutes

## Ingredients

### Bruschetta
- 2 tablespoons olive oil
- 4 Roma tomatoes, diced
- 2 tablespoons finely chopped red onion
- 1/4 cup thinly sliced fresh basil
- 1/2 teaspoon minced garlic
- 1 tablespoon balsamic vinegar
- 1/4 teaspoon salt
- Pinch black pepper

### Spice Blend
- 1/2 cup almond flour
- 1 teaspoon Italian seasoning
- 1/2 teaspoon salt
- 1/4 teaspoon black pepper
- 1/4 teaspoon garlic powder

### Chicken
- 4 boneless, skinless chicken breasts
- Salt and black pepper to taste
- 2 tablespoons olive oil
- 1/2 cup shaved Parmesan cheese
- 1 tablespoon balsamic vinegar glaze

## Instructions

1. Combine the bruschetta ingredients in a medium bowl and set aside.

2. Combine the spice blend ingredients in a wide, shallow bowl.

3. Salt and pepper the chicken breasts. One at a time, place the chicken breasts between 2 sheets of parchment and pound to a 1/2-inch thickness.

4. Dredge the chicken breasts in the spice blend.

5. Heat the oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat, swirling to cover the bottom.

6. Reduce the heat to medium and add the chicken. Cook undisturbed for 2 minutes.

7. Flip the chicken and reduce the heat to low. Cover and cook undisturbed for 10 minutes.

8. Turn off the heat, but wait an additional 10 minutes before removing the lid.

9. Check that the chicken is fully cooked; there should be no pink and a thermometer should register at least 165 degrees F.

10. Top the chicken breasts with equal portions of the bruschetta and shaved Parmesan cheese.

11. Drizzle with the balsamic glaze before serving.

## Tips

- Make sure to let the chicken rest covered for the full 10 minutes after turning off heat to ensure it cooks through completely
- Use a meat thermometer to ensure chicken reaches 165°F internal temperature for food safety
- Try making Bruschetta Meatzza Pizza using an ultra-low-carb crust topped with this bruschetta and balsamic glaze for a creative variation
