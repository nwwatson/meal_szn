# Crunchy Chicken Milanese

A crispy, ketogenic-friendly chicken dish featuring thin-pounded chicken breasts coated in coconut flour and crushed pork rinds for maximum crunch

## Source
The Easy 5-Ingredient Ketogenic Diet Cookbook 

## Servings
2 servings

## Prep Time
10 minutes

## Cook Time
10 minutes

## Ingredients

- 2 boneless skinless chicken breasts
- 1/2 cup coconut flour
- 1 teaspoon ground cayenne pepper
- Pink Himalayan salt to taste
- Freshly ground black pepper to taste
- 1 egg, lightly beaten
- 1/2 cup crushed pork rinds
- 2 tablespoons olive oil

## Instructions

1. Pound the chicken breasts with a heavy mallet until they are about 1/2 inch thick. (If you don't have a kitchen mallet, you can use the thick rim of a heavy plate.)

2. Prepare two separate prep plates and one small, shallow bowl: On plate 1, put the coconut flour, cayenne pepper, pink Himalayan salt, and pepper. Mix together. Crack the egg into the small bowl, and lightly beat it with a fork or whisk. On plate 2, put the crushed pork rinds.

3. In a large skillet over medium-high heat, heat the olive oil.

4. Dredge 1 chicken breast on both sides in the coconut-flour mixture. Dip the chicken into the egg, and coat both sides. Dredge the chicken in the pork-rind mixture, pressing the pork rinds into the chicken so they stick. Place the coated chicken in the hot skillet and repeat with the other chicken breast.

5. Cook the chicken for 3 to 5 minutes on each side, until brown, crispy, and cooked through, and serve.

## Tips

- You can replace the cayenne pepper with grated Parmesan cheese if you prefer milder food
- Press the pork rinds firmly into the chicken to ensure they stick during cooking
- Make sure the oil is hot before adding the chicken to achieve maximum crispiness
