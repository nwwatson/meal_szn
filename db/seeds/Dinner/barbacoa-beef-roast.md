# Barbacoa Beef Roast

A simplified 5-ingredient version of Chipotle-style barbacoa that's packed with flavor and falls apart after slow cooking for 8 hours

## Source
The Easy 5-Ingredient Ketogenic Diet Cookbook 

## Servings
2 servings

## Prep Time
10 minutes

## Cook Time
8 hours

## Ingredients

- 1 pound beef chuck roast
- Pink Himalayan salt to taste
- Freshly ground black pepper to taste
- 4 chipotle peppers in adobo sauce (from 12-ounce can)
- 1 (6-ounce) can green jalapeño chiles
- 2 tablespoons apple cider vinegar
- 1/2 cup beef broth

## Instructions

1. With the crock insert in place, preheat the slow cooker to low.

2. Season the beef chuck roast on both sides with pink Himalayan salt and pepper. Put the roast in the slow cooker.

3. In a food processor or blender, combine the chipotle peppers and their adobo sauce, jalapeños, and apple cider vinegar, and pulse until smooth. Add the beef broth, and pulse a few more times. Pour the chile mixture over the top of the roast.

4. Cover and cook on low for 8 hours.

5. Transfer the beef to a cutting board, and use two forks to shred the meat.

6. Serve hot.

## Tips

- You can substitute beef brisket for the chuck roast for a different texture
- Save the leftover adobo sauce from the chipotle can for future recipes - it keeps well in the refrigerator
- The shredded meat is perfect for keto-friendly tacos, salad bowls, or eating on its own
