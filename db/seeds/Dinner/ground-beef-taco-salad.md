# Ground Beef Taco Salad

A hearty low-carb taco salad with seasoned ground beef, fresh vegetables, and Mexican-inspired toppings

## Source
How Not To Get Sick

## Servings
4 servings

## Prep Time
10 minutes

## Cook Time
15 minutes

## Ingredients

- 1 tablespoon olive oil
- 1 teaspoon minced garlic
- 1 pound 90% lean ground beef
- 2 tablespoons taco seasoning
- 1/4 cup water
- 1 head romaine lettuce, chopped
- 1 cup cherry tomatoes, halved
- 2 tablespoons guacamole
- 1/2 cup chopped green onion
- 1/4 cup chopped fresh cilantro
- 1 cup thinly sliced jicama
- 1 (2.25-ounce) can sliced black olives, drained
- 1/2 cup shredded cheddar cheese
- Sour cream, for garnish (optional)
- Tajin seasoning, for garnish

## Instructions

1. Heat the oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat. Add the garlic and sauté until the garlic is fragrant and golden, about 2 minutes.

2. Add the ground beef and cook, breaking it up with a wooden spoon, until no longer pink, about 8 minutes.

3. Drain off any excess grease and liquid. Return the skillet to medium heat and add the taco seasoning and water to the ground beef.

4. Stir to fully combine and bring to a simmer. Once all of the liquid has been absorbed, remove the skillet from the heat and set aside.

5. In 4 salad bowls, evenly divide the lettuce, tomatoes, guacamole, green onion, cilantro, jicama, olives, and ground beef.

6. Top with the shredded cheddar cheese, a dollop of sour cream (if using), and a sprinkle of Tajin.

## Tips

- Use a wooden spoon to break up the ground beef while cooking to ensure even browning and prevent large chunks
- Let the taco-seasoned beef cool slightly before adding to the salad to prevent wilting the lettuce
- For extra crunch and freshness, chill all salad ingredients before assembling the bowls
