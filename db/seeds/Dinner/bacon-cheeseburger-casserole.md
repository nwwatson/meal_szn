# Bacon Cheeseburger Casserole

A hearty, protein-filled keto casserole with bacon, ground beef, eggs, and cheese that heats up wonderfully as leftovers

## Source
The Easy 5-Ingredient Ketogenic Diet Cookbook 

## Servings
4 servings

## Prep Time
10 minutes

## Cook Time
50 minutes

## Ingredients

### For the Bacon and Ground Beef
- 1 pound bacon
- 1 tablespoon ghee
- 1 pound ground beef
- Pink Himalayan salt to taste
- Freshly ground black pepper to taste

### For the Casserole
- 1 tablespoon ghee
- 1/2 cup heavy whipping cream
- 4 large eggs, lightly beaten
- 3/4 cup shredded cheese (Mexican blend)
- Pink Himalayan salt to taste
- Freshly ground black pepper to taste

## Instructions

1. In a large skillet over medium-high heat, cook the bacon on both sides until crispy, about 8 minutes. Transfer the bacon to a paper towel-lined plate to drain and cool for 5 minutes. Transfer to a cutting board and chop the bacon.

2. In a second large skillet over medium-high heat, heat the ghee. Add the ground beef and season with pink Himalayan salt and pepper. Stir occasionally, breaking the beef chunks apart.

3. Once the meat is browned, after about 8 minutes, drain the fat and mix in the chopped bacon.

4. Preheat the oven to 350°F. Coat a 9-by-13-inch baking dish with the ghee.

5. Spoon the meat-and-bacon mixture into the baking dish as a first layer.

6. In a medium bowl, mix together the cream, eggs, and half of the cheese, and season with pink Himalayan salt and pepper. Pour over the meat. Top with the remaining half of the cheese.

7. Bake for 30 minutes, or until the cheese on top is melted and lightly browned.

8. Let the casserole sit for 5 minutes on a cooling rack before cutting and serving.

## Tips

- You can substitute ground turkey for ground beef if desired
- Add 1/2 cup of diced pickles to the beef mixture for acidity to balance the richness of the dish
- Try adding 1/4 cup of low-sugar tomato sauce like Rao's brand to the cream and egg mixture for extra flavor
