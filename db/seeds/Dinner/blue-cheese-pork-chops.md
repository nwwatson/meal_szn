# Blue Cheese Pork Chops

Quick and easy pork chops topped with a rich blue cheese sauce, perfect for the keto diet.

## Source
The Easy 5-Ingredient Ketogenic Diet Cookbook 

## Servings
2 servings

## Prep Time
5 minutes

## Cook Time
10 minutes

## Ingredients

- 2 boneless pork chops
- Pink Himalayan salt to taste
- Freshly ground black pepper to taste
- 2 tablespoons butter
- 1/3 cup blue cheese crumbles
- 1/4 cup heavy whipping cream
- 1/3 cup sour cream

## Instructions

1. Pat the pork chops dry, and season with pink Himalayan salt and pepper.

2. In a medium skillet over medium heat, melt the butter. When the butter melts and is very hot, add the pork chops and sear on each side for 3 minutes.

3. Transfer the pork chops to a plate and let rest for 3 to 5 minutes.

4. In a medium saucepan over medium heat, melt the blue cheese crumbles, stirring frequently so they don't burn.

5. Add the cream and the sour cream to the pan with the blue cheese. Let simmer for a few minutes, stirring occasionally.

6. For an extra kick of flavor in the sauce, pour the pork-chop pan juice into the cheese mixture and stir. Let simmer while the pork chops are resting.

7. Put the pork chops on two plates, pour the blue cheese sauce over the top of each, and serve.

## Tips

- The blue cheese sauce is also delicious poured over vegetables.
- Make sure to let the pork chops rest after cooking to retain their juices.
- Stir the blue cheese frequently while melting to prevent burning and ensure a smooth sauce.
