# Butter and Herb Pork Chops

Simple and delicious pork chops with herbs, butter, and olive oil that bake quickly and can go from kitchen to dinner table in no more than 30 minutes

## Source
The Easy 5-Ingredient Ketogenic Diet Cookbook 

## Servings
2 servings

## Prep Time
5 minutes

## Cook Time
25 minutes

## Ingredients

- 1 tablespoon butter, plus more for coating
- 2 boneless pork chops
- Pink Himalayan salt to taste
- Freshly ground black pepper to taste
- 1 tablespoon dried Italian seasoning
- 1 tablespoon chopped fresh flat-leaf Italian parsley
- 1 tablespoon olive oil

## Instructions

1. Preheat the oven to 350°F. Choose a baking dish that will hold both pork chops and coat it with butter.

2. Pat the pork chops dry with a paper towel, place them in the prepared baking dish, and season with pink Himalayan salt, pepper, and Italian seasoning.

3. Top with the fresh parsley, drizzle the olive oil over both pork chops, and top each chop with 1/2 tablespoon of butter.

4. Bake for 20 to 25 minutes. Thinner pork chops will cook faster than thicker ones.

5. Place the pork chops on two plates, spoon the buttery juices over the meat, and serve hot.

## Tips

- This dish is particularly delicious with mashed cauliflower
- Pat the pork chops completely dry before seasoning for better browning and flavor
- Adjust cooking time based on thickness of pork chops - thinner chops will cook faster
