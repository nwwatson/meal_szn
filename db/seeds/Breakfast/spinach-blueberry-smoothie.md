# Spinach-Blueberry Smoothie

A nutritious smoothie packed with antioxidants from blueberries and spinach, perfect for a healthy breakfast or snack

## Source
The Complete Ketogenic Guide for Beginners 

## Servings
2

## Prep Time
5

## Cook Time
0

## Ingredients

- 1 cup coconut milk
- 1 cup spinach
- 1/2 English cucumber, chopped
- 1/2 cup blueberries
- 1 scoop plain protein powder
- 2 tablespoons coconut oil
- 4 ice cubes
- Mint sprigs, for garnish

## Instructions

1. Put the coconut milk, spinach, cucumber, blueberries, protein powder, coconut oil, and ice in a blender and blend until smooth.

2. Pour into 2 glasses, garnish each with the mint, and serve immediately.

## Tips

- Look for organic blueberries as they have higher antioxidant levels than conventionally grown fruit
- For a creamier texture, freeze the blueberries ahead of time instead of using ice cubes
- Add the spinach first to the blender to ensure it gets fully blended and doesn't leave green chunks
