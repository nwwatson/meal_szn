# Creamy Keto Chia Pudding

A delicious and easy overnight chia pudding that's perfect for meal-prepping your weekday breakfast. With only 1.5g net carbs per portion, it's the perfect low carb breakfast, snack or dessert.

## Source
Sugar Free Londoner (https://sugarfreelondoner.com/keto-chia-pudding/)

## Servings
4 servings

## Prep Time
5 minutes

## Cook Time
0 minutes (1 hour 10 minutes resting time)

## Ingredients

### Chia Pudding Base
- 1/2 cup chia seeds (8 tbsp)
- 1 3/4 cups unsweetened almond milk (420 ml)
- 1/2 teaspoon vanilla extract
- 1/2 cup coconut yoghurt or Greek yoghurt (120g)
- 1/2-2 tablespoons sugar-free syrup or powdered sweetener of choice (optional)

## Instructions

1. In a bowl or glass jar, add the chia seeds, almond milk, vanilla and optional low-carb sweetener and stir well. If using a mason jar, add the lid and shake to combine.

2. Let it sit for about 10 minutes, then stir, shake, or massage to break up any chia clumps.

3. Stir in the yogurt. Cover and place the pudding in the fridge to thicken for 1-2 hours or overnight.

4. Serve with your favorite toppings such as berries, nuts, coconut flakes, or a drizzle of almond butter.

## Tips

- For a looser pudding, add 1/4 cup (60 ml) of almond milk after soaking
- The pudding can be stored in the refrigerator for up to 5 days, making it perfect for weekly meal prep
- Try topping with fresh berries, cacao powder, almond butter, toasted nuts, or coconut flakes for variety
- Use coconut yoghurt for a dairy-free version, or Greek yoghurt for extra protein
