# Egg-in-a-Hole Breakfast Burger

A fun and rebellious breakfast burger featuring gooey eggs cooked inside ground beef patties, topped with crispy bacon, melted Cheddar cheese, and spicy Sriracha Mayo

## Source
The Easy 5-Ingredient Ketogenic Diet Cookbook 

## Servings
2 servings

## Prep Time
5 minutes

## Cook Time
15 minutes

## Ingredients

- 4 bacon slices
- 6 ounces ground beef
- Pink Himalayan salt
- Freshly ground black pepper
- 2 tablespoons butter
- 2 large eggs
- 2 slices Cheddar cheese
- 1 tablespoon Sriracha Mayo

## Instructions

1. In a large skillet over medium-high heat, cook the bacon on both sides until crispy, about 8 minutes. Transfer the bacon to a paper towel-lined plate.

2. Form the ground beef into two burger patties. Use a small glass or cookie cutter to cut out the middle of each (like a donut). Take the cut-out meat and add it to the edges of the two burgers. Season the meat with pink Himalayan salt and pepper.

3. In the skillet still set over medium-high heat, melt the butter. Add the burger patties, cook the first side for 2 minutes, and then flip.

4. Crack an egg into the middle of each burger, and cook until the whites are set, 1 to 2 minutes. Season the eggs with pink Himalayan salt and pepper.

5. Top each egg with 1 slice of Cheddar cheese, turn the heat off, and cover the skillet to melt the cheese, about 2 minutes.

6. Transfer the burgers to two plates, top each with two bacon slices and some of the Sriracha Mayo, and serve.

## Tips

- Use Vital Farms Pasture-Raised Eggs for the best quality and flavor
- You can replace the Cheddar cheese with avocado and the butter with olive oil if you are not eating dairy
- If you haven't made Sriracha Mayo, you can substitute with Spicy Red Pepper Miso Mayo which is dairy-free
