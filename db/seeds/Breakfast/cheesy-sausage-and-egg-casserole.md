# Cheesy Sausage and Egg Casserole

A hearty breakfast casserole with ground pork sausage, eggs, and Colby jack cheese with colorful bell peppers

## Source
How Not To Get Sick

## Servings
8 servings

## Prep Time
15 minutes

## Cook Time
30 minutes

## Ingredients

- 8 large eggs
- 1/4 cup heavy cream
- 1/2 teaspoon salt
- 1/4 teaspoon black pepper
- 1 cup shredded Colby jack cheese, divided
- 1 tablespoon unsalted butter
- 1 tablespoon minced garlic
- 1 pound ground pork sausage
- 1/2 cup diced green bell pepper
- 1/2 cup diced red bell pepper
- 4 green onions, chopped
- Magic Sauce (page 135), for serving (optional)

## Instructions

1. Preheat the oven to 400 degrees F. Grease a 9-inch baking pan with butter.

2. In a large mixing bowl, whisk together the eggs, cream, salt, and pepper until fully combined. Mix in 1/2 cup of cheese. Set aside.

3. Melt the butter in a medium skillet over medium-high heat. Add the garlic and cook until fragrant, about 2 minutes.

4. Add the sausage and cook, breaking it up with a wooden spoon, until browned, about 5 minutes.

5. Add the bell peppers and green onions and cook until the vegetables are soft, about 3 minutes.

6. Transfer the sausage mixture to the prepared baking pan and spread into an even layer.

7. Pour the egg mixture over the sausage mixture. Sprinkle the remaining 1/2 cup of cheese over the top.

8. Bake for 25 minutes, or until cooked through and golden.

9. Cool for 10 minutes, then slice and serve, with sauce if desired.

## Tips

- Let the casserole cool for the full 10 minutes before slicing to help it set properly and make cleaner cuts
- Pre-cook the sausage mixture the night before and store in the refrigerator to make morning assembly even quicker
- This casserole can be assembled ahead of time and refrigerated overnight - just add 5-10 minutes to the baking time if cooking from cold
