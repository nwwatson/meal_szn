# Creamy Cinnamon Smoothie

A keto-friendly smoothie featuring warm cinnamon spice with creamy coconut milk and vanilla protein powder

## Source
The Complete Ketogenic Guide for Beginners 

## Servings
2 servings

## Prep Time
5 minutes

## Cook Time
0 minutes

## Ingredients

- 2 cups coconut milk
- 1 scoop vanilla protein powder
- 5 drops liquid stevia
- 1 teaspoon ground cinnamon
- 1/2 teaspoon alcohol-free vanilla extract

## Instructions

1. Put the coconut milk, protein powder, stevia, cinnamon, and vanilla in a blender and blend until smooth.

2. Pour into 2 glasses and serve immediately.

## Tips

- Try to find Ceylon cinnamon instead of Cassia, because it does not contain coumarin, a toxin that affects the liver. Ceylon cinnamon is lighter in color and has a more delicate flavor than Cassia.
- You can find vanilla extract without alcohol in specialty stores, or use imitation vanilla extract if alcohol-free vanilla is not available.
- For best texture, make sure your coconut milk is well-shaken before using and serve the smoothie immediately for optimal consistency.
