# Fluffy Pancakes

Low-carb keto-friendly pancakes made with almond flour and served with fresh fruit

## Source
How Not To Get Sick

## Servings
4 servings

## Prep Time
10 minutes

## Cook Time
15 minutes

## Ingredients

- 1 1/2 cups almond flour
- 1 teaspoon baking powder
- 1 tablespoon granular Swerve
- 1/4 teaspoon salt
- 3 large eggs
- 1/3 cup heavy cream
- 1/4 teaspoon vanilla extract
- 1 tablespoon unsalted butter, melted, plus more for greasing (optional)
- Sliced fresh fruit or berries, for serving (optional)
- Zero-sugar syrup, for serving (optional)

## Instructions

1. Preheat a griddle or large skillet over medium-high heat.

2. In a mixing bowl, whisk together the almond flour, baking powder, Swerve, salt, eggs, heavy cream, and vanilla.

3. Stir in the melted butter until well combined.

4. Grease the griddle with additional butter or coconut oil. Reduce the heat to low.

5. Working in batches as necessary, use a 1/4 cup measure to scoop the batter onto the griddle, leaving space between the pancakes.

6. When bubbles form (after 2-3 minutes), flip the pancakes to cook on the other side for 2-3 minutes more.

7. Serve with fresh fruit and a drizzle of syrup if desired.

## Tips

- Look for zero-sugar syrups sweetened with natural sweeteners like stevia, monk fruit, or erythritol to replace traditional maple syrup.
- Keep the heat on low once you start cooking to prevent burning the almond flour pancakes.
- Let the batter rest for 2-3 minutes before cooking to allow the almond flour to fully hydrate for fluffier pancakes.
