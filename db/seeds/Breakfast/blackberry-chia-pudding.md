# Blackberry-Chia Pudding

A nutritious keto-friendly pudding made with coconut milk, blackberries, and chia seeds that sets overnight. Perfect for breakfast or dessert, packed with fiber, omega-3s, and healthy fats.

## Source
The Easy 5-Ingredient Ketogenic Diet Cookbook 

## Servings
2 servings

## Prep Time
10 minutes

## Cook Time
0 minutes

## Ingredients

- 1 cup unsweetened full-fat coconut milk
- 1 teaspoon liquid stevia
- 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
- 1/2 cup blackberries, fresh or frozen (no sugar added if frozen)
- 1/4 cup chia seeds

## Instructions

1. In a food processor or blender, process the coconut milk, stevia, and vanilla until the mixture starts to thicken.

2. Add the blackberries and process until thoroughly mixed and purple. Fold in the chia seeds.

3. Divide the mixture between two small cups with lids, and refrigerate overnight or up to 3 days before serving.

## Tips

- A food processor or blender is essential for this recipe - whisking by hand will not thicken the mixture properly.
- The pudding can be stored in the refrigerator for up to 3 days, making it perfect for meal prep.
- The chia seeds help slow digestion and the high fat content keeps you feeling satisfied for hours.
