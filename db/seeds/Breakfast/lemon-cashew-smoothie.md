# Lemon-Cashew Smoothie

A luscious keto smoothie that combines cashew milk and heavy cream for a tart, refreshing, and satisfying breakfast or snack

## Source
The Complete Ketogenic Guide for Beginners 

## Servings
1 serving

## Prep Time
5 minutes

## Cook Time
0 minutes

## Ingredients

- 1 cup unsweetened cashew milk
- 1 scoop plain protein powder
- 1/4 cup heavy whipping cream
- 1 tablespoon coconut oil
- 1/4 cup freshly squeezed lemon juice
- 1 teaspoon sweetener

## Instructions

1. Put the cashew milk, heavy cream, lemon juice, protein powder, coconut oil, and sweetener in a blender and blend until smooth.

2. Pour into a glass and serve immediately.

## Tips

- Add a few ice cubes to make it like a rich citrus sorbet instead of a regular smoothie
- Add a couple leaves of fresh mint to enhance the fresh flavor
- Try substituting almond milk or coconut milk for cashew milk to create different flavor profiles
