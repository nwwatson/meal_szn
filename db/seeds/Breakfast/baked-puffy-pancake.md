# Baked Puffy Pancake

A golden, puffy keto-friendly pancake baked in a cast-iron skillet with a fluffy texture and crispy edges

## Source
How Not To Get Sick

## Servings
4 servings

## Prep Time
10 minutes

## Cook Time
20 minutes

## Ingredients

- 3 large eggs
- 1/4 cup heavy cream
- 1/4 cup full-fat sour cream
- 4 tablespoons (1/2 stick) unsalted butter, melted, divided
- 1 1/2 cup almond flour
- 2 tablespoons coconut flour
- 1 teaspoon baking powder
- 1/4 teaspoon salt
- 3 tablespoons granular Swerve
- 1 1/2 teaspoons vanilla extract
- 1/2 teaspoon grated lemon zest
- Confectioners Swerve, for serving (optional)
- Fresh raspberries, for serving (optional)
- Zero-sugar syrup, for serving (optional)

## Instructions

1. Preheat the oven to 425 degrees F. Place an 8-inch cast-iron skillet in the oven while it preheats.

2. In a food processor, blend the eggs, heavy cream, sour cream, and 1 tablespoon of melted butter. Add the almond flour, coconut flour, baking powder, salt, granular Swerve, vanilla, and lemon zest. Blend until smooth, scraping down the sides as needed.

3. Using pot holders, remove the hot skillet from the oven. Being careful to hold the skillet away from you, add 2 tablespoons of butter to the skillet and swirl to coat the entire bottom. The butter may spatter.

4. Pour the batter into the skillet and return it to the oven. Bake for 10 to 15 minutes, until there is no longer any jiggling in the center of the pancake.

5. Turn on the broiler and cook for 5 minutes, until the top is golden. Remove the skillet from the oven and immediately brush the pancake with the remaining 1 tablespoon of melted butter.

6. If desired, top with confectioners Swerve and a handful of raspberries, then cut into 4 wedges and drizzle with syrup.

## Tips

- Make sure to preheat the cast-iron skillet in the oven - this helps create the signature puffy texture and prevents sticking
- Be very careful when adding butter to the hot skillet as it will spatter - hold the pan away from your body
- Don't skip the final butter brushing step as it adds flavor and helps create a beautiful golden finish
