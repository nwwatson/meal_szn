# Cream Cheese and Coconut Flour Pancakes or Waffles

Authentic, puffy ketogenic pancakes or waffles made with cream cheese and coconut flour that can be prepared on a skillet, griddle, or waffle iron

## Source
The Easy 5-Ingredient Ketogenic Diet Cookbook 

## Servings
2 servings (6 pancakes or 3 waffles)

## Prep Time
5 minutes

## Cook Time
10 minutes

## Ingredients

- 4 large eggs
- 4 ounces cream cheese, at room temperature
- 1 teaspoon liquid stevia
- 1 1/2 teaspoons baking powder
- 4 tablespoons coconut flour
- 4 tablespoons butter, divided
- Nonstick cooking spray (for waffles)

## Instructions

1. In a food processor (or blender), process the eggs, cream cheese, stevia, baking powder, and coconut flour until thoroughly combined.

2. Mix in any desired add-ins from the variations.

3. For pancakes: In a large skillet over medium-high heat, melt 2 tablespoons of butter, turning the skillet to spread it evenly in the bottom of the pan.

4. Pour the batter into the skillet in 1/4-cup portions for 3 (4-inch) pancakes.

5. The pancakes will get puffy when it is time to flip them, after about 2 minutes. Cook for about 1 minute more, until lightly browned on the bottom.

6. Repeat with the remaining 2 tablespoons of butter for the remaining batter.

7. For waffles: Preheat the closed waffle iron until it's nice and hot on medium-high heat.

8. Open your waffle iron, and spray the top and bottom with nonstick cooking spray.

9. Pour in the batter in three portions, to not quite cover the waffle-iron surface; don't get it close to the edges, or it will spill out when it cooks.

10. Waffles are done when they quit steaming and are lightly browned and crisp.

11. Serve hot.

## Tips

- Make sure cream cheese is at room temperature for easier blending and smoother batter consistency.
- For variations, try adding 2 teaspoons vanilla extract with a sprinkle of cinnamon, berries, chocolate chips, or even crushed pork rinds for extra texture.
- Heat your syrup with 1 tablespoon of butter for added richness - warm syrup is much better than cold syrup on these pancakes and waffles.
