# Garlic Aioli

A fancy-sounding but easy-to-make garlic aioli sauce with fresh herbs that's perfect for keto diets

## Source
The Easy 5-Ingredient Ketogenic Diet Cookbook 

## Servings
4 servings

## Prep Time
5 minutes

## Cook Time
0 minutes

## Ingredients

- 1/2 cup mayonnaise
- 2 garlic cloves, minced
- Juice of 1 lemon
- 1 tablespoon chopped fresh flat-leaf Italian parsley
- 1 teaspoon chopped chives
- Pink Himalayan salt to taste
- Freshly ground black pepper to taste

## Instructions

1. In a food processor (or blender), combine the mayonnaise, garlic, lemon juice, parsley, and chives, and season with pink Himalayan salt and pepper. Blend until fully combined.

2. Pour into a sealed glass container and chill in the refrigerator for at least 30 minutes before serving. This sauce will keep in the fridge for up to 1 week.

## Tips

- Mince the garlic as finely as possible for best results. You can even grate it with a zester if you have one.
- Allow the aioli to chill for at least 30 minutes before serving to let the flavors meld together.
- The chives and parsley are optional but add wonderful fresh herb flavor to the sauce
