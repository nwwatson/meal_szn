# Peanut Sauce

A creamy, flavorful peanut sauce perfect for chicken dishes, zoodles, or adding Asian flavor to any meal

## Source
The Easy 5-Ingredient Ketogenic Diet Cookbook 

## Servings
4 servings

## Prep Time
5 minutes

## Cook Time
0 minutes

## Ingredients

- 1/2 cup creamy peanut butter
- 2 tablespoons soy sauce (or coconut aminos)
- 1 teaspoon Sriracha sauce
- 1 teaspoon toasted sesame oil
- 1 teaspoon garlic powder

## Instructions

1. In a food processor (or blender), blend the peanut butter, soy sauce, Sriracha sauce, sesame oil, and garlic powder until thoroughly mixed.

2. Pour into an airtight glass container and keep in the refrigerator for up to 1 week.

## Tips

- For added texture, use chunky peanut butter instead of creamy
- Store in an airtight glass container in the refrigerator for up to 1 week
- Use coconut aminos instead of soy sauce for a soy-free alternative
