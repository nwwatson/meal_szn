# Chunky Blue Cheese Dressing

A great dressing for wedge salads or steak salads, but perfect for hot wings! This cold and creamy blue cheese dressing is chunky and flavorful.

## Source
The Easy 5-Ingredient Ketogenic Diet Cookbook 

## Servings
4 servings

## Prep Time
5 minutes

## Cook Time
0 minutes

## Ingredients

- 1/2 cup sour cream
- 1/2 cup mayonnaise
- Juice of 1/2 lemon
- 1/2 teaspoon Worcestershire sauce
- Pink Himalayan salt to taste
- Freshly ground black pepper to taste
- 2 ounces crumbled blue cheese

## Instructions

1. In a medium bowl, whisk the sour cream, mayonnaise, lemon juice, and Worcestershire sauce.

2. Season with pink Himalayan salt and pepper, and whisk again until fully combined.

3. Fold in the crumbled blue cheese until well combined.

4. Keep in a sealed glass container in the refrigerator for up to 1 week.

## Tips

- You can adjust the amount of blue cheese crumbles to your preference - this recipe makes it chunky
- Store in a sealed glass container in the refrigerator for up to 1 week
- This dressing is perfect for hot wings, wedge salads, or drizzled over grilled steak
