# Dijon Vinaigrette

A light, tangy dressing that pairs especially well with salads containing tomatoes, berries, or other sweet elements

## Source
The Easy 5-Ingredient Ketogenic Diet Cookbook 

## Servings
4 servings

## Prep Time
5 minutes

## Cook Time
0 minutes

## Ingredients

- 2 tablespoons Dijon mustard
- Juice of 1/2 lemon
- 1 garlic clove, finely minced
- 1 1/2 tablespoons red wine vinegar
- Pink Himalayan salt to taste
- Freshly ground black pepper to taste
- 3 tablespoons olive oil

## Instructions

1. In a small bowl, whisk the mustard, lemon juice, garlic, and red wine vinegar until well combined. Season with pink Himalayan salt and pepper, and whisk again.

2. Slowly add the olive oil, a little bit at a time, whisking constantly.

3. Keep in a sealed glass container in the refrigerator for up to 1 week.

## Tips

- Feel free to replace the red wine vinegar with apple cider vinegar for a different flavor profile
- Add the olive oil slowly while whisking constantly to create a proper emulsion
- Store in a sealed glass container in the refrigerator for up to 1 week for best freshness
