# Sriracha Mayo

A creamy and spicy sauce that makes any food better, perfect for dipping chicken, veggies, and anything else you can grab

## Source
The Easy 5-Ingredient Ketogenic Diet Cookbook 

## Servings
4 servings

## Prep Time
5 minutes

## Cook Time
0 minutes

## Ingredients

- 1/2 cup mayonnaise
- 2 tablespoons Sriracha sauce
- 1/2 teaspoon garlic powder
- 1/2 teaspoon onion powder
- 1/4 teaspoon paprika

## Instructions

1. In a small bowl, whisk together the mayonnaise, Sriracha, garlic powder, onion powder, and paprika until well mixed.

2. Pour into an airtight glass container, and keep in the refrigerator for up to 1 week.

## Tips

- You can adjust the amount of Sriracha sauce to increase or decrease the spice level
- Store in an airtight glass container in the refrigerator for up to 1 week
- This sauce pairs perfectly with keto-friendly foods like grilled chicken, roasted vegetables, or low-carb wraps
